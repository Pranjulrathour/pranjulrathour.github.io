Can Copilot create images? Editing photos, timing, PPT, Excel, Power BI, agents and Gemini

Key takeaways

  • Microsoft Copilot can generate images from text prompts using image models from OpenAI and Microsoft.
  • It can edit and restyle images and create new images based on uploaded photos, within content policies.
  • Images usually take seconds to under a minute; demand and complexity affect timing.
  • Microsoft 365 Copilot creates PowerPoint decks, builds Excel formulas and tables, and helps create Power BI reports.
  • Build agents with Copilot Studio or the agent builder in Microsoft 365 Copilot; Gemini also generates images.

Yes, Microsoft Copilot can create images: describe what you want, and it generates pictures using image models from OpenAI and Microsoft, which you can refine, restyle and download. Microsoft 365 Copilot also creates PowerPoint decks, builds Excel tables and formulas, and helps make Power BI reports, while Copilot Studio builds agents. I build AI products across several providers, so this page covers Copilot images and where they come from, editing, timing, Office features, Power BI, agents, and Gemini's image generation.

Can Copilot create images?

Yes. In the Copilot app, on the web, in Windows and in Microsoft Edge, you can ask Copilot to create an image from a description, such as "a watercolour of students coding at night", and it returns generated images you can refine with follow-up prompts.

For better results, describe the subject, setting, style, lighting, composition and aspect ratio, and say what to avoid. Microsoft Designer, integrated with Copilot, adds design templates for social posts and invitations. Usage limits and speed depend on your account and plan. Generated images follow Microsoft's content policies, so requests involving real people, violence or copyrighted characters may be refused. Students often use it for presentation visuals, event posters and project mock-ups, saving time compared with searching stock image sites.

Where do Copilot images come from?

Copilot images are generated by AI image models, including models from OpenAI and Microsoft's own image models, not copied from the internet. The models learned visual patterns during training and create new images from your prompt.

Microsoft adds provenance information, such as content credentials, to indicate images are AI-generated. Because models learned from large datasets, outputs can sometimes resemble existing styles, so avoid prompts imitating specific living artists or brands for commercial use. Check Microsoft's terms for how you may use generated images, especially in business or published materials.

Can Copilot edit images?

Yes. Copilot can modify images it created, and you can upload images and ask for changes such as changing the style, background, colours or adding elements, within content policies. Microsoft Designer and Photos apps provide further AI editing tools like background removal and generative erase.

Editing works best with specific instructions: "keep the person and pose, change the background to a library, warm lighting". Results may alter details you wanted to keep, so iterate step by step. For precise professional edits, combine AI tools with traditional editors. Only edit images you have rights to, and do not create misleading edits of real people.

Can Copilot create images from photos?

Yes, you can upload a photo and ask Copilot to create a new image based on it, for example restyling it as an illustration or using it as a reference for composition. Results are new generations, not exact edits, so details may change.

Uses include turning product photos into stylised marketing images, creating cartoon versions of scenes, or generating variations of a design. Privacy matters: avoid uploading photos of other people without consent, and remember content policies may limit edits of identifiable faces. For accurate product photos, real photography or careful editing remains more reliable than generation.

How long does Copilot take to generate images?

Usually from a few seconds to under a minute per request. Time depends on demand, the model used, image complexity and your account's priority; during busy periods it can take longer or hit usage limits.

If generation stalls, refresh, simplify the prompt or try again later. Paid plans may offer faster or more generations. For many images, generating one at a time with refinements tends to give better results than asking for many at once. Developers needing image generation in apps typically use image APIs from providers directly, with their own rate limits.

Can Copilot create a PPT?

Yes. Copilot in PowerPoint, part of Microsoft 365 Copilot, can create a presentation from a prompt or from a Word document, generating slides with titles, text, images and speaker notes, and can summarise or restyle existing decks.

Also asked as: can copilot create ppt

Give it structure: audience, purpose, number of slides and key points, or a source document. Use your organisation's template for consistent branding. Review every slide for accuracy and add your own examples and data. The free Copilot app can draft outlines and content you paste into PowerPoint, but direct deck creation inside PowerPoint requires a qualifying Microsoft 365 plan.

Can Copilot create Excel sheets?

Copilot in Excel can create formulas, add calculated columns, build PivotTables and charts, highlight data and explain results from your instructions, working best with data formatted as tables. It can also help build spreadsheets from descriptions in newer versions.

Example prompts: "Add a column calculating profit margin", "Show total sales by region as a chart", "Highlight overdue invoices". Check formulas on a few rows, since AI can misinterpret columns. For complex models, ask Copilot to explain each formula so you understand and can maintain the workbook. Requires a qualifying Microsoft 365 plan and files saved in OneDrive or SharePoint. Without it, you can paste sample data into the free Copilot chat and ask for formulas to copy into Excel yourself.

Can Copilot create a Power BI dashboard?

Yes, Copilot in Power BI can suggest and create report pages from prompts, summarise data, write DAX measures and answer questions about your data, depending on your organisation's capacity and licensing.

Also asked as: can copilot create power bi dashboard

Prompts like "Create a page showing sales trends by region and top products" generate visuals you can refine. Copilot works best with a clean, well-named data model; unclear column names and missing relationships produce poor results. Review generated measures and visuals for correctness before sharing dashboards. Admins must enable Copilot features in Power BI or Fabric settings.

Can I create AI agents in Microsoft Copilot?

Yes. Use the agent builder in Microsoft 365 Copilot for simple agents grounded in your files and sites, or Copilot Studio for more advanced agents with custom topics, actions, connectors, triggers and publishing to Teams, websites and other channels.

Start with a narrow agent, such as an HR policy assistant using SharePoint documents. Test with real questions, refine instructions, and add actions, such as creating tickets, only with appropriate permissions. Organisations should govern agents with admin controls, data policies and monitoring. Licensing affects which features are available. Measure whether the agent answers correctly on a test set of real questions before rolling it out widely, and review conversation logs in the first weeks.

Will Gemini create images?

Yes. Google's Gemini app creates and edits images from text prompts and uploaded photos using Google's image models, and Gemini models are available to developers for image generation through Google's APIs.

Gemini is especially good at conversational editing: upload a photo and describe changes step by step while keeping other details consistent. Generated images include SynthID watermarks marking them as AI-made. Usage limits depend on your plan. As with any image tool, respect consent and copyright, and avoid misleading images of real people.
