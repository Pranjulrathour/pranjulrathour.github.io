Key takeaways

  • The certification tests building LLM applications on Databricks: design, data preparation, RAG, deployment, governance and evaluation.
  • A generative AI engineer builds products on top of language and image models: retrieval, agents, evaluation and deployment.
  • Prepare with the official exam guide, Databricks Academy's GenAI engineering course and hands-on practice in a workspace.
  • It is moderate for people with RAG experience and hard for those who only know theory or have never used Databricks.
  • Certifications help, but a deployed GenAI project with evaluation results carries more weight with employers.

The Databricks Certified Generative AI Engineer Associate is a certification that tests whether you can design, build, deploy and govern LLM-powered applications on the Databricks platform, especially retrieval-augmented generation with Vector Search, model serving and evaluation. It suits engineers working in Databricks-based companies. I build RAG systems such as RAG.NextUpgrad, so this page explains the certification, the generative AI engineer role and work, courses and a roadmap, the study guide, preparation, and how hard the exam is.

What is the Databricks Certified Generative AI Engineer Associate?

It is a Databricks certification validating skills in building generative AI solutions on Databricks: decomposing problems into LLM application designs, preparing data and chunking documents, building RAG chains and agents, using Vector Search and model serving, applying governance, and evaluating and monitoring applications.

Also asked as: what is databricks certified generative ai engineer associate · databricks certified generative ai engineer associate study guide · databricks certified generative ai engineer associate course · how to prepare for databricks certified generative ai engineer associate · how hard is databricks certified generative ai engineer associate

The exam is a proctored, multiple-choice test, taken online or at a test centre, with around 45 questions in 90 minutes according to recent exam guides, and the certification is valid for two years. Databricks recommends hands-on experience with its generative AI tools before attempting it. Check the official certification page for the current exam guide, format and policies, since details are updated.

What is the generative AI engineer role?

A generative AI engineer builds applications and systems on top of generative models, especially large language models: chatbots, RAG assistants, document processing, agents and content tools, taking them from prototype to reliable production.

Also asked as: what is generative ai engineer role

The role blends software engineering with AI-specific skills: retrieval, prompt and context design, evaluation, fine-tuning when needed, safety, cost control and deployment. It differs from research roles that create new models, and overlaps with ML engineering and backend development. Employers look for people who can show working systems, measure their quality and explain trade-offs. Titles vary: the same work may be called AI engineer, LLM engineer, applied AI engineer or machine learning engineer, so read job descriptions for the actual responsibilities.

What work does a generative AI engineer do?

Day to day, a generative AI engineer designs LLM features, builds ingestion and retrieval pipelines, writes and tests prompts, integrates tools and APIs, evaluates outputs against test sets, deploys services, monitors quality and cost, and works with product teams on requirements and risks.

Also asked as: what is the work of generative ai engineer

Typical tasks include chunking and indexing documents, tuning hybrid search and reranking, adding citations and refusal rules, building agent workflows, fine-tuning small models with LoRA, setting up guardrails against prompt injection, and investigating failures from production logs. In my work, this ranges from RAG.NextUpgrad's retrieval pipeline to schema validation in DocuLens AI and QLoRA training in FineTune Studio.

What is a good generative AI engineer course?

Good options include DeepLearning.AI's short courses on LLM apps, RAG, agents and evaluation; Hugging Face courses on transformers and agents; cloud provider learning paths such as Databricks Academy, Google Cloud and Microsoft Learn; and university courses on NLP for foundations.

Choose courses with hands-on projects, current tools and evaluation content, not just prompting tips. Combine one structured course with your own project: build a RAG assistant over real documents, then add evaluation, then deploy it. Certificates show effort, but a working, documented project demonstrates the skills employers hire for. Revisit your course choices every few months, since generative AI tools change quickly and older courses may teach deprecated interfaces.

What is the Databricks generative AI engineer course?

Databricks Academy offers a "Generative AI Engineering with Databricks" learning path covering generative AI solution design, data preparation for RAG, building applications with Vector Search and Mosaic AI tools, deployment with model serving, and evaluation and governance, aligned with the certification.

It is available as self-paced online training and instructor-led sessions, and Databricks partners and customers may have access to more content. Work through it inside a real Databricks workspace, practising each step rather than only watching. The course maps closely to the exam's sections, making it the most direct preparation resource.

What is the roadmap to become a generative AI engineer?

Start with Python and software engineering, then learn LLM fundamentals, prompting and structured output, embeddings and RAG, evaluation, agents and tool use, fine-tuning basics, and deployment with monitoring. Build a project at each stage.

Also asked as: roadmap to become generative ai engineer

A concise roadmap:

  • Months 1 to 2: Python, APIs, Git, SQL, a web framework.
  • Months 3 to 4: LLM APIs, prompting, a first RAG app.
  • Months 5 to 6: hybrid retrieval, reranking, evaluation sets.
  • Months 7 to 8: agents, tools, MCP, guardrails.
  • Months 9 to 10: fine-tuning with QLoRA, deployment, cost control.
  • Ongoing: hackathons, open source, a polished portfolio.

Add a platform certification, such as Databricks, if your target employers use it.

What is the study guide for the Databricks GenAI Engineer Associate?

The official exam guide on the Databricks certification page is the study guide: it lists exam sections, objectives, recommended training and sample questions. Use it as a checklist, mapping each objective to documentation and hands-on practice.

Sections typically cover designing applications, data preparation, application development, assembling and deploying applications, governance, and evaluation and monitoring. For each, know the relevant Databricks features: Vector Search index types, model serving endpoints, Unity Catalog governance, agent and evaluation tools, and MLflow tracking. Supplement with documentation pages for each feature and build a small end-to-end RAG app in a workspace.

What is the Databricks GenAI Engineer certification course structure?

The official preparation path combines the Databricks Academy generative AI engineering courses with the exam guide and practice in a workspace. Courses are organised around the exam domains, from solution design through data preparation, development, deployment, governance and evaluation.

Many learners supplement official material with practice questions and community notes, but rely on official sources for accuracy, since unofficial dumps can be outdated or wrong and violate exam policies. Sharing or using leaked exam questions can also lead to certification being revoked. Plan your study as modules: one week per domain, ending each week by building that part of a project, then a final week of review and practice questions.

How do I prepare for the Databricks GenAI Engineer Associate?

Read the exam guide, complete the Databricks Academy generative AI course, then build an end-to-end RAG application in a Databricks workspace: ingest documents, create a Vector Search index, build a chain or agent, deploy it with model serving, and evaluate it.

Preparation tips:

  • Know chunking strategies and when to use each.
  • Understand Vector Search index types and syncing.
  • Learn model serving options and how to choose models.
  • Study governance with Unity Catalog and guardrails.
  • Practise evaluation concepts and MLflow tracking.
  • Take official sample questions and review mistakes.

Hands-on practice matters most, because scenario questions test applied judgement.

How hard is the Databricks GenAI Engineer Associate exam?

It is moderately difficult. Engineers who have built RAG systems and used Databricks usually find it manageable with a few weeks of focused preparation. Those with only theoretical knowledge or no Databricks experience find it harder, because questions are scenario-based and platform-specific.

The challenge is choosing the best option among plausible ones, which requires understanding trade-offs in chunking, retrieval, model choice, deployment and evaluation. General RAG experience helps a lot, but learn Databricks-specific features and terminology. Budget practice time in a real workspace; it turns abstract objectives into concrete knowledge.
