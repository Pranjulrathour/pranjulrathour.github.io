Is Microsoft AI certification free? Free courses, AI-900 and the roadmap

Microsoft's AI learning is free on Microsoft Learn; exams usually cost money, with free vouchers at times. The AI certifications, free routes and a roadmap.

Key takeaways

  • Microsoft's AI learning paths on Microsoft Learn are free; certification exams usually have a fee.
  • Free routes to a credential include Microsoft Applied Skills assessments, student programmes and occasional free exam vouchers from training events.
  • Azure AI Fundamentals is the entry certification; role-based certifications such as Azure AI Engineer come next.
  • Students get extra help: Azure for Students credits and discounted exam pricing.
  • Pair any certification with a project you built on Azure; that is what interviews test.

Microsoft's AI training is free: Microsoft Learn offers learning paths, modules and practice assessments at no cost. Most certification exams, such as Azure AI Fundamentals, have a fee, but there are free ways to earn credentials, including Microsoft Applied Skills assessments, student programmes and free exam vouchers that Microsoft has offered through training events. This page lists Microsoft's AI certifications, the free courses and routes, how to get a free certification, and a sensible roadmap.

Is Microsoft AI certification free?

The learning is free, but most certification exams are not. Microsoft Learn's AI courses and practice assessments cost nothing; the proctored exams for certifications such as Azure AI Fundamentals and Azure AI Engineer Associate normally require a fee, with discounts for students.

There are exceptions. Microsoft Applied Skills credentials are earned through free, lab-based assessments. Microsoft has run training events, such as Virtual Training Days and skills challenges, that offer free or discounted exam vouchers to people who complete them. Some colleges and partner programmes provide vouchers too. Check Microsoft Learn and official event pages for what is currently available rather than relying on social media posts.

What are Microsoft's AI certifications?

Microsoft's AI credentials include Azure AI Fundamentals for beginners, Azure AI Engineer Associate for building AI solutions on Azure, Azure Data Scientist Associate for machine learning, and Applied Skills credentials for specific AI tasks. Microsoft also adds new AI credentials as its platform changes.

Also asked as: what are microsoft ai certifications

In brief:

  • Azure AI Fundamentals: concepts of AI, machine learning, computer vision, language and generative AI on Azure. No coding required.
  • Azure AI Engineer Associate: building solutions with Azure AI services and Azure OpenAI, with code.
  • Azure Data Scientist Associate: training and deploying ML models with Azure Machine Learning.
  • Applied Skills: narrow, hands-on credentials, such as building a generative AI solution.

Check the credentials catalogue for current exam codes and retirements.

Is the Azure AI Fundamentals certification free?

The Azure AI Fundamentals learning path is free on Microsoft Learn, and so is the practice assessment. The certification exam normally has a fee, though Microsoft has offered free vouchers through training events, and students get discounted pricing.

Also asked as: is microsoft azure ai fundamentals certification free

The fundamentals exam suits students, business users and developers new to AI on Azure. Preparation typically takes a week or two with the free learning path and practice assessment. Fundamentals certifications do not expire. If you attend an official training event that offers a voucher, read the conditions: vouchers usually have deadlines and apply only to specific exams.

What is the free AI certification course by Microsoft?

Microsoft Learn hosts free AI courses and learning paths, including the Azure AI Fundamentals path, generative AI modules, and paths for building with Azure AI services. Completing modules earns free badges and trophies on your Learn profile, which are not certifications.

Also asked as: microsoft ai training free course

Useful free paths for students:

  • Introduction to AI concepts and responsible AI.
  • Fundamentals of generative AI and Azure OpenAI.
  • Building retrieval-augmented generation solutions on Azure.
  • Computer vision and language services.

Microsoft has also partnered with LinkedIn on free AI learning paths with certificates of completion at times. A certificate of completion shows you finished a course; a certification shows you passed a proctored exam, so be clear about which you list on a resume.

Is there an official free Microsoft AI certification course?

The official free courses are on Microsoft Learn, Microsoft's own training site. Be cautious with third-party sites or videos claiming "official free Microsoft certification"; the certification itself comes only through Microsoft's exam process or Applied Skills assessments.

Also asked as: official) microsoft free artificial intelligence certification course

To verify that a course is official, check that it is on learn.microsoft.com or on a Microsoft partner page linked from there. Official certifications appear in your Microsoft Learn profile and can be shared as verified credentials. If a site asks for payment to "unlock" a free Microsoft certificate, or asks for your Microsoft password outside Microsoft's login page, avoid it.

What free Microsoft AI training courses are available?

Free options include Microsoft Learn learning paths and modules, Microsoft's open-source curricula on GitHub, such as beginner courses on generative AI and AI agents, and recorded sessions from Microsoft events.

A practical free plan:

  • Microsoft Learn's AI fundamentals path for concepts.
  • A generative AI path covering prompts, retrieval and responsible AI.
  • Microsoft's GitHub beginner courses on generative AI and agents for hands-on code.
  • Azure for Students credits to build a small project on Azure.

Building something, such as a document Q&A assistant with Azure AI Search and a model endpoint, turns free training into evidence you can show employers.

Does Microsoft provide free certification?

Yes, in some forms. Microsoft Applied Skills credentials are free to earn through hands-on assessments, and Microsoft periodically offers free exam vouchers for certifications through events and programmes. Standard certification exams otherwise have a fee.

Applied Skills focus on specific tasks, such as building an AI solution or developing a generative AI app, and you complete them in a lab environment online. They are verified credentials that appear on your Microsoft Learn profile. They suit students who want proof of practical skills without paying exam fees. Check the Applied Skills catalogue for current AI-related options.

Does Microsoft offer free certification exam vouchers?

Microsoft has offered free or discounted exam vouchers through official training events, such as Virtual Training Days and Cloud Skills Challenges, through student programmes, and through some academic and partner initiatives. Availability changes.

How to find them: watch the Microsoft Learn events pages, join official student communities, and ask your college whether it participates in Microsoft academic programmes. Read voucher terms carefully: eligible exams, expiry dates and registration steps. Avoid buying vouchers from unofficial resellers; they may be invalid or obtained improperly, and exam providers can cancel them.

How do I get a Microsoft certification for free?

Combine free learning with a free credential route: complete Microsoft Learn paths, then either earn an Applied Skills credential, or attend an official event that offers a free exam voucher and take the exam before it expires.

Also asked as: how to get microsoft free certification

Steps:

  • Create a Microsoft Learn profile.
  • Complete the free learning path for your target exam and the practice assessment.
  • Check Applied Skills for a free, related credential.
  • Watch official events for voucher offers.
  • Use student discounts if you pay for an exam.
  • Build a small project on Azure with student credits to apply what you learned.

Add credentials to LinkedIn with the verification link, and mention the project in interviews.

What is a good Microsoft AI certification roadmap?

Start with Azure AI Fundamentals for concepts, build a project, then move to Azure AI Engineer Associate if you want to build AI solutions, or Azure Data Scientist Associate if you want to train models. Add Applied Skills credentials for specific tasks along the way.

Also asked as: ai certification roadmap microsoft

A roadmap for students:

  • Step 1: AI fundamentals learning path and exam, or an Applied Skills credential.
  • Step 2: Python, APIs and a RAG project using Azure AI services.
  • Step 3: Azure AI Engineer Associate preparation with hands-on labs.
  • Step 4: deploy a portfolio project with monitoring and cost limits.

Certifications help with screening, especially at Microsoft partners and IT services firms; projects decide interviews.
