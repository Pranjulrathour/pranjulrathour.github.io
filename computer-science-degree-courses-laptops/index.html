Key takeaways

  • CS degrees take three to four years, with MCA and M.Tech adding two years.
  • Pre-CS is a provisional status at some universities before admission to the full major.
  • Pair free courses such as CS50 and NPTEL with classic books and real projects.

A computer science degree takes three to four years in most countries, and the best results come from pairing coursework with strong courses, books, projects and a clear CV. Laptop and graduate school choices follow your goals. I am a BCA student who leads engineering at SCULT INDIA and mentors students, so this page covers degree duration, pre-CS, courses, books, SAP, CVs, case studies, Mac versus Windows, MacBook Air versus Pro, and top graduate programmes.

How long is a computer science degree course?

In India, B.Tech in computer science takes four years, BCA and B.Sc three years, or four with honours under the National Education Policy, and MCA or M.Tech two years. In the United States, a bachelor's usually takes four years.

Also asked as: computer science degree course duration

In the United Kingdom, most computer science bachelor's degrees take three years, with integrated master's options taking four. Integrated five-year programmes combine bachelor's and master's degrees at some Indian institutes. Diplomas in computer engineering take about three years and can lead to lateral entry into the second year of B.Tech. Part-time and online degrees, such as those from IGNOU or IIT Madras's online BS programme, offer flexible timelines for working learners.

What is the difference between pre-computer science and computer science?

At many universities, especially in the United States, pre-computer science is a provisional status for students who want to major in computer science but must first complete prerequisite courses and meet grade requirements before admission to the competitive major.

Also asked as: pre computer science vs computer science

Pre-CS students take introductory programming, discrete mathematics and calculus, then apply or qualify to declare the full major. If they do not meet requirements, they may choose related majors such as information science or data science. Full computer science majors take advanced courses in algorithms, systems and specialisations. In India, admission usually happens directly into the programme through entrance exams, so pre-CS status is uncommon. Check your university's catalogue for specific rules and deadlines.

What is the best course for a computer science student?

Excellent free courses include Harvard's CS50 for foundations, MIT OpenCourseWare's algorithms and systems courses, Stanford's machine learning lectures, NPTEL courses from the IITs, and fast.ai for practical deep learning.

Also asked as: best course for computer science student

Choose courses that fill gaps in your syllabus and match your goals. For placements, add data structures practice and system design. For AI careers, combine machine learning fundamentals with LLM application courses from DeepLearning.AI or Hugging Face. For web development, follow a project-based full-stack path. Complete one course fully with all assignments rather than starting many. Pair every course with a project that applies its concepts to a problem you care about.

What are the best books for computer science students?

Classic books include Introduction to Algorithms by Cormen and colleagues, Structure and Interpretation of Computer Programs, Computer Systems: A Programmer's Perspective, Operating System Concepts, and Designing Data-Intensive Applications by Martin Kleppmann.

For practical programming, The Pragmatic Programmer and Clean Code offer habits that last a career, though some advice is debated. For machine learning, Hands-On Machine Learning by Aurélien Géron is widely used, and Build a Large Language Model (From Scratch) by Sebastian Raschka explains modern AI. Read actively: implement examples, solve exercises and summarise chapters. One deeply studied book teaches more than skimming ten. Many classics are available through college libraries.

Is an SAP course useful for computer science students?

It can be. SAP skills, such as ABAP programming, SAP Business Technology Platform, Fiori development and S/4HANA, are in demand at consulting firms and large enterprises that run SAP systems, offering a distinct career path.

Also asked as: sap course for computer science students

SAP Learning offers free learning journeys, and official certifications validate skills, though exams carry fees. Computer science students suit technical roles, such as ABAP or BTP development and integration, more than functional modules like finance or supply chain. Before committing, check job listings at your target companies, since demand varies. SAP careers can be stable and well regarded, but they tie you to one ecosystem, so keep general programming skills strong as well.

What are good CV examples for computer science students?

A strong CS student CV fits on one page and includes education, technical skills, three or four projects with links and measurable results, internships, achievements such as hackathons, and leadership or community roles.

Write project bullets with action, technology and outcome, for example: built a RAG assistant with hybrid search and reranking in FastAPI, answering questions from course notes with cited sources. Avoid long skill lists without evidence. Use a clean, ATS-friendly format with standard headings. Tailor the CV to each role, moving relevant projects to the top. Hackathon wins belong in an achievements section with the event, result and year. Ask mentors or seniors to review it.

What are case study examples for computer science students?

Useful case studies include designing a URL shortener or chat system, analysing how UPI or Aadhaar handle massive scale, reviewing a well-documented outage or data breach, and studying how recommendation systems or RAG assistants are built.

A good case study states the problem, constraints, design choices, trade-offs, results and lessons. For system design practice, compare your design with published engineering blogs from companies. For security, read public incident reports and identify root causes and fixes. For AI, document a project such as a document question-answering system, including evaluation results and failure cases. Writing case studies of your own projects strengthens portfolios and prepares you for interview discussions.

Mac vs Windows: which is better for computer science students?

Both work well. Macs offer a Unix-based terminal, excellent battery life and are required for iOS app development. Windows laptops offer more hardware choice, NVIDIA GPUs for CUDA-based machine learning, and Linux tools through WSL.

Also asked as: mac vs windows for computer science students

Choose based on your goals and college requirements. Some university software and engineering tools run only on Windows, so check your syllabus. For AI work with CUDA, a Windows or Linux laptop with an NVIDIA GPU helps, though cloud notebooks cover much learning. For web and backend development, either platform is fine. Whatever you choose, learn Linux basics, since servers run Linux. Aim for at least 16 GB of RAM on any platform.

MacBook Air vs MacBook Pro: which suits a computer science student?

A MacBook Air with an Apple silicon chip and 16 GB of memory handles most computer science coursework, web development and light machine learning. A MacBook Pro suits heavier work, such as running local LLMs, large builds or video editing.

Also asked as: macbook air vs macbook pro computer science student

The Pro's active cooling sustains performance during long compiles or training runs, and higher memory configurations let you run larger local models through unified memory. The Air is lighter, silent and has excellent battery life. Prioritise memory over processor upgrades, since memory cannot be upgraded later. Students should also consider education discounts and refurbished models. If you mainly use cloud GPUs for AI, the Air is usually enough.

What are the best computer science graduate programmes in the world?

Top programmes consistently include MIT, Stanford, Carnegie Mellon and UC Berkeley, alongside ETH Zurich, Oxford, Cambridge, Tsinghua and the National University of Singapore. In India, IISc and the IITs lead for postgraduate computer science.

Also asked as: best computer science graduate programs in the world

Rankings differ by method: QS and Times Higher Education weigh reputation, while CSRankings measures research publications by area, which helps if you want a specific field such as AI or systems. The best programme for you depends on research fit, advisers, funding, location and career goals. For research degrees, contact potential advisers whose work interests you. Strong applications show research experience, projects, recommendation letters and a clear statement of purpose.
