Will ChatGPT shut down? Outages, Projects and RAG, images, watermarks, health and energy

Most problems people hit, outages, image failures or weak answers, have specific causes and fixes. I build AI products with multi-provider fallback, such as RAG.NextUpgrad, so this page covers what ChatGPT is best for, shutdown rumours, outages, Projects and whether they use RAG, image generation and watermarks, medical advice, and ChatGPT's environmental impact.

What is ChatGPT best used for?

ChatGPT is best for drafting and editing text, explaining concepts, brainstorming, summarising documents, writing and debugging code, analysing data with code, translating, and planning tasks. It is a strong first-draft and thinking partner.

It is weaker where precision and accountability matter without verification: exact citations, recent facts without search, legal or medical decisions, and arithmetic in long reasoning chains. Use web search or uploaded documents for current or specific information, and check important claims. Students get the most value by asking it to explain and quiz rather than to produce finished assignments. Professionals benefit from giving it context, examples and a clear format for the output they want.

Will ChatGPT shut down?

There is no sign that ChatGPT will shut down. OpenAI continues to release new models and features, and ChatGPT has hundreds of millions of weekly users. Rumours usually come from model retirements, outages or misleading social media posts.

What does change is which models are available: OpenAI retires older models, as it did when GPT-4 was removed from ChatGPT in 2025, and introduces new defaults. Features also move between plans. If your workflow depends on a specific model's behaviour, keep prompts and outputs documented so you can adapt. For applications, build in flexibility to switch providers, since any service can change terms or availability, which is why multi-provider fallback is good engineering.

Is ChatGPT down right now?

Check OpenAI's official status page first: it lists incidents affecting ChatGPT, the API and specific features such as image generation or voice. Outage trackers and social media can confirm whether others see the same problem.

If the status page shows no incident, the problem is likely local: refresh, log out and in, clear the site's cache, disable browser extensions, try a private window or another browser, update the app, or switch networks and turn off VPNs. Partial outages often affect one feature, such as file uploads or images, while text chat works. During major incidents, wait rather than retrying repeatedly, and use an alternative assistant for urgent work.

What are ChatGPT Projects?

Projects in ChatGPT are workspaces that group related chats, uploaded files and custom instructions around one topic, such as a course, research paper or product. Chats inside a project share that context automatically.

Use a project for ongoing work: upload reference documents once, set instructions such as tone or format, and start new chats that already know the background. Projects can also reference earlier chats within the same project, which helps continuity. They are useful for students organising subjects and professionals running long tasks. Keep files current, remove outdated versions to avoid confusion, and avoid uploading sensitive data unless your plan and settings meet your privacy requirements.

Do ChatGPT Projects use RAG?

Effectively, yes. When project files are too large to fit into the conversation, ChatGPT searches them and pulls relevant passages into context, a retrieval-augmented approach. Smaller files may be read more fully.

This explains common behaviour: ChatGPT may miss details in a long file if its search does not retrieve the right section, and it answers better when you name the document or section you mean. For precise work, ask it to quote the passage it used, and verify. For applications needing guaranteed coverage, auditable citations and control over chunking and ranking, build your own RAG pipeline, as I did with RAG.NextUpgrad, rather than relying on a chat product's retrieval.

Why is ChatGPT not generating images?

Common reasons: you reached the image limit for your plan, the request conflicts with content policies, image generation is temporarily degraded, or the chat or model you are using does not support it. Error messages usually hint at which.

Fixes: check the status page for image incidents; start a new chat and ask explicitly to create an image; rephrase prompts that might be read as violating policy, such as requests involving real people or copyrighted characters; wait for limits to reset; and update the app or try the web version. Long, complex prompts can time out, so simplify first, then refine with follow-up edits. If Gemini fails similarly, the causes and fixes are much the same.

Which ChatGPT is best for images?

Use the image generation built into ChatGPT, which runs on OpenAI's latest GPT image models and is available across plans with different limits. Paid plans offer more generations and faster processing.

For understanding images, such as reading a chart or explaining a photo, any current ChatGPT model with vision works, with reasoning models better at complex analysis. For generating images, ChatGPT handles text in images, editing and iterative refinement well. For developers, the OpenAI API exposes image models for programmatic generation and editing. Compare with alternatives such as Gemini, Midjourney and open models when you need specific styles, and check usage rights for commercial projects.

Do ChatGPT images have watermarks?

ChatGPT images generally have no visible watermark, but they carry C2PA metadata, a provenance standard that records they were created with OpenAI's tools. Tools that read Content Credentials can show this information.

Metadata is easy to lose: screenshots, re-saving and many social platforms strip it, so its absence does not prove an image is human-made. OpenAI and other companies also research invisible watermarking and detection classifiers, but none is perfect. For honest use, label AI-generated images in your work, especially in journalism, education and advertising. If you need to verify an image, check Content Credentials with a verification tool, but combine that with other evidence.

Should ChatGPT be used for medical advice?

Use ChatGPT to understand medical information, prepare questions for your doctor or learn about a diagnosis, not as a replacement for professional care. It can be wrong, lacks your full history, and cannot examine you.

It can explain test terminology, summarise general guidelines and help you organise symptoms before an appointment. It should not decide medication changes, interpret urgent symptoms or replace emergency services. Language models can state errors confidently, so verify information with trusted health sources and clinicians. Protect privacy: avoid sharing identifiable health details unless you understand how they are stored. If symptoms are severe or sudden, contact a doctor or emergency services immediately rather than asking a chatbot.

Why is ChatGPT considered bad for the environment?

Running AI models requires data centres that consume electricity and water for cooling, and training large models uses significant energy. As usage grows, total consumption grows, which raises concerns about emissions and local water use.

Also asked as: why is chatgpt bad for the environment

Per-query estimates vary: in 2025, OpenAI's CEO stated an average ChatGPT query uses about 0.34 watt-hours, while the International Energy Agency projects global data centre electricity demand to roughly double by 2030, with AI a major driver. Impact depends on the electricity source and cooling method. Individuals can reduce waste by avoiding unnecessary regenerations; companies can choose efficient models, cache results and use cleaner power. Smaller, task-specific models often meet needs with far less energy.
