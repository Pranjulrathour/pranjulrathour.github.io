Best pitch deck template: slides, structure, Canva, and presenting a pitch

What a pitch deck includes, how many slides, the best template structure, making one in Canva, decks for investors and hackathons, and how to present it.

Key takeaways

  • A strong pitch deck tells one story: problem, solution, demo, market, business model, traction, team and ask.
  • Ten to twelve slides suit investors; five to eight suit a hackathon pitch of three to five minutes.
  • Templates help with layout, but clarity, one idea per slide and real evidence win the room.
  • Canva, PowerPoint, Google Slides and Keynote all work; export a PDF for sharing.
  • Rehearse the pitch aloud with a timer, and keep a recorded demo as backup.

The best pitch deck template is a simple structure that tells one clear story: the problem, your solution, a demo or proof, the market, how you make money or create impact, traction, the team and what you are asking for. Design matters less than clarity and evidence. I have pitched and won Product Genesis at Vividhotsava 2025 with BrandHive, along with two other hackathon first prizes, and judged other teams, so this page covers what a deck includes, slide counts and length, creating one, investor versus hackathon decks, Canva, format and how to present.

What is the best pitch deck template?

The best template follows a proven order and keeps one idea per slide: title, problem, solution, demo or product, market, business model or impact, traction, competition, team, and the ask. Many founders adapt the structures published by well-known investors and accelerators.

A template that works for students and early founders:

  • Title: name, one-line description, logo.
  • Problem: a specific user and their pain, with evidence.
  • Solution: what you built, in one sentence.
  • Demo: screenshots or a live demo.
  • Market: who and how many.
  • Business model or impact.
  • Traction: users, pilots, results.
  • Competition: why you are different.
  • Team: why you can build it.
  • Ask: funding, mentorship, partnership or next step.

Large, readable text and one strong visual per slide beat crowded designs.

What does a pitch deck include?

A pitch deck includes the problem, solution, product or demo, target market, business model, traction, competition, team and the ask. For hackathons, replace business details with impact, technical approach and next steps as the judging criteria require.

Also asked as: what does pitch deck include

Each slide should answer a question the audience has: why does this matter, why now, why you, how will it grow, and what do you want from us. Include real evidence where you have it: user quotes, pilot results, a working prototype. Avoid long paragraphs, tiny charts and jargon; if a slide needs a long explanation, it needs to be simpler.

How many slides should a pitch deck have?

For investors, ten to twelve main slides, with extra details in an appendix. For a hackathon pitch of three to five minutes, five to eight slides, because each slide needs time to land.

Also asked as: how many slides pitch deck

A rule of thumb is roughly one slide per thirty to sixty seconds of speaking. Fewer, stronger slides are better than many rushed ones. Keep backup slides for likely questions, such as technical architecture, data privacy or detailed financials, and show them only if asked. Judges and investors remember the story and the demo, not the slide count.

How long should a pitch be?

Hackathon pitches are usually three to five minutes plus questions; investor pitches commonly run ten to twenty minutes; a quick verbal pitch should be under a minute. Always confirm the time limit and rehearse to fit it with margin.

Also asked as: how long pitch deck

Plan time deliberately: about a fifth on the problem, the largest share on the solution and demo, and a short close on impact, traction and the ask. Rehearse with a timer at least three times; most first drafts run long. Cut slides rather than speaking faster. Leaving time for questions often helps, because questions show what the audience cares about.

How do I create a pitch deck?

Write the story first, then build slides. Draft a one-paragraph narrative, outline one slide per point, gather evidence and visuals, design simply, and rehearse with feedback.

Also asked as: how to create pitch deck

Steps:

  • Write the problem and solution in one sentence each.
  • List the questions your audience will ask and answer each on a slide.
  • Add proof: demo screenshots, user feedback, data.
  • Use a clean template with large fonts and consistent colours.
  • Practise aloud, get feedback from someone outside your team, and simplify.

Use AI tools to tighten wording or suggest structure, but make sure every claim is true and you can explain every number.

How do I make a pitch deck for investors?

Focus on the opportunity and your ability to capture it: a painful problem, a solution customers want, evidence of traction, a large enough market, a credible business model, a strong team, and a clear funding ask with how you will use it.

Also asked as: how to make pitch deck for investors

Investor-specific tips:

  • Lead with traction if you have it; it is the strongest evidence.
  • Show market size with a clear, bottom-up calculation.
  • Explain how you will acquire customers and what it costs, in principle.
  • Address competition honestly and state your advantage.
  • State the round size and the milestones it will reach.

Send a PDF version that reads well without you presenting, and keep a separate, more visual version for live pitches.

Can I create a pitch deck on Canva?

Yes. Canva has many pitch deck templates and makes it easy to design clean slides, add images and charts, collaborate with teammates, and present or export to PDF or PowerPoint.

Tips for Canva decks: pick a minimal template and remove decorative elements, use one or two fonts, keep text large, and use your own product screenshots rather than stock images where possible. Export a PDF for sharing, and test the presentation on the actual screen or projector if you can. PowerPoint, Google Slides and Keynote work equally well; choose the one your team collaborates in most easily.

Is a pitch deck a PPT?

A pitch deck is a presentation, and it can be made in PowerPoint, which saves files as PPTX, or in Google Slides, Keynote or Canva. "PPT" is often used loosely to mean any slide presentation.

Also asked as: is pitch deck a ppt

When sharing, a PDF is usually best, because it looks the same on every device and cannot be accidentally edited. Some competitions, such as Smart India Hackathon, specify a template and format for idea submissions, so follow official instructions exactly. For live pitches, keep the presentation file and a PDF backup on your laptop and a USB drive or cloud link.

How do I give a pitch?

Open with a hook about the problem, keep eye contact, tell the story slide by slide in your own words, show the demo confidently, close with your impact and ask, then handle questions calmly and concisely.

Also asked as: how to give pitch deck

Presenting tips from the judge's table:

  • Start with the user and their problem, not your technology stack.
  • Rehearse the demo many times; have a recorded backup.
  • Assign speaking parts so transitions are smooth.
  • Do not read slides; slides support you.
  • Answer questions directly, admit unknowns, and say how you would find out.

Teams that clearly explain what they built and why it matters usually outscore teams with more features and a confusing story.

Is pitch deck one word or two words?

"Pitch deck" is two words. It means a short slide presentation used to pitch a business, product or idea to investors, judges, customers or partners.

You will also see "pitch presentation" or simply "deck" used in startup conversations, such as "send us your deck". In writing, keep it as two words without a hyphen when used as a noun, as in "our pitch deck", and some writers hyphenate it when used before another noun, as in "pitch-deck template", though "pitch deck template" without a hyphen is common and acceptable.
