Text to speech guide: engines, AI voices, getting started, Android, Edge, TikTok, Copilot

What text to speech engines are, whether voices are AI, how to start, Android, Edge, TikTok, Copilot, why it helps and speech to text issues.

Key takeaways

  • A TTS engine normalises text, predicts pronunciation and prosody, and generates audio with neural models.
  • Most modern natural voices are AI-generated, so voice cloning needs consent and disclosure.
  • Free text to speech is built into Edge, Android, iPhone, Mac and Windows.
  • TikTok and Copilot include text to speech, while APIs and Piper serve developers.
  • Speech to text fails on noise, accents, jargon and mixed languages, so review transcripts.

Text to speech, or TTS, converts written text into spoken audio, and modern neural voices now sound close to human. It is built into phones, browsers and operating systems, and available through apps and developer APIs. My OCR & Speech Workspace handles the opposite direction with live speech-to-text, and KrishGyan delivered farming advice in farmers' own language over WhatsApp, so this page covers TTS engines, AI voices, getting started, where to get TTS, Android, Edge, TikTok, Copilot, why it helps and why speech to text struggles.

What is a text to speech engine?

A text to speech engine is the software that turns text into audio. It normalises text, such as numbers and abbreviations, converts words to pronunciation, predicts rhythm and intonation, and generates a waveform using a neural model or older synthesis methods.

Also asked as: what is text to speech engine

Operating systems include engines that apps can call: Android uses Speech Services by Google by default, Windows provides its own voices to Narrator and apps, and Apple devices use built-in voices for VoiceOver and Spoken Content. Modern engines use neural networks, building on research such as DeepMind's WaveNet, to produce natural prosody. Cloud engines offer many voices and languages through APIs, while open-source engines such as Piper run offline on modest hardware. Engines differ in naturalness, latency, language coverage and licensing.

Are text to speech voices AI?

Most modern ones are. Today's natural-sounding voices are generated by neural networks trained on recordings of human speech. Older voices used rule-based formant synthesis or stitched together recorded sound fragments, which sounded robotic.

Neural TTS learns pronunciation, rhythm and emotion from data, and some systems can clone a voice from a short sample. That power raises ethical questions: voice cloning without consent enables fraud and impersonation, so reputable providers require permission and add safeguards. Many platforms label AI-generated audio or embed watermarks. When you publish content with synthetic voices, disclose it where platforms or laws require. Simple screen readers still use lighter-weight voices, prioritising speed and clarity at high speaking rates over naturalness.

How do you start using text to speech?

Start with the free features already on your devices: Read aloud in Microsoft Edge, Select to Speak on Android, Speak Selection on iPhone and Mac, and Narrator on Windows. Turn them on in accessibility settings, then select text and play it.

Also asked as: how to start text to speech

Adjust voice, speed and language until listening feels comfortable; many people prefer slightly faster speeds after a few days. For documents and articles, browser read-aloud features work well. If you need audio files, such as voiceovers, use a TTS app or online service that exports MP3. Developers can start with the browser's Web Speech API, which speaks text with a few lines of JavaScript, or cloud APIs from major providers for higher-quality voices. Try several voices before committing to one for a project.

Where can you get text to speech?

You can get TTS built into Windows, macOS, iOS, Android and browsers such as Edge and Chrome, from apps and websites for creators, and from developer APIs and open-source engines for building your own products.

Also asked as: where to get text to speech

Cloud APIs include Google Cloud Text-to-Speech, Azure AI Speech, Amazon Polly, OpenAI's speech models and ElevenLabs, with many voices and languages, including Indian languages. Open-source options such as Piper, Coqui XTTS and Kokoro run locally for privacy and offline use. Creator tools such as CapCut and TikTok include TTS inside video editors. Accessibility tools such as screen readers and reading assistants offer TTS designed for long listening. Check licensing for commercial use, since some voices and free tiers restrict how audio may be used.

How do you use text to speech on Android?

Turn on Select to Speak in Settings, Accessibility, then tap its icon and select any text on screen to hear it read aloud. Choose the voice engine, language, speed and pitch under the text-to-speech output settings.

Also asked as: how to speech text on android

Speech Services by Google is the default engine on most phones, and you can download higher-quality voices and additional languages, including Hindi and other Indian languages. Samsung phones also include Samsung's own engine. For full screen reading, TalkBack reads everything you touch and is designed for blind and low-vision users. Many apps, such as Chrome and Play Books, offer their own read-aloud options. If voices sound robotic, update Speech Services and select a newer voice. Menu names vary slightly between manufacturers and Android versions.

How do you use text to speech in Microsoft Edge?

Open a web page or PDF in Edge, then click Read aloud in the address bar or right-click and choose Read aloud, or press Ctrl, Shift and U. A toolbar lets you pause, skip, change voice and adjust speed.

Also asked as: how to text to speech in edge

Edge offers many natural-sounding voices across languages, which makes it one of the easiest free ways to listen to articles and documents. To read only part of a page, select text first and then choose Read aloud selection. Immersive Reader mode removes clutter and highlights words as they are read, helping readers with dyslexia follow along. Read aloud works on PDFs opened in Edge too, which is useful for study material. Some natural voices need an internet connection, while local voices work offline.

How do you use text to speech on TikTok?

In the TikTok editor, tap Text, type your words and tap Done, then tap the text box and choose Text-to-speech. Pick a voice, and TikTok reads the text aloud in your video at that point.

Also asked as: how to speech text on tiktok

You can adjust when the text appears and how long it stays by dragging its timing on the timeline, and the voice follows the text. Different voices suit different moods, and availability varies by region and language. Keep text short, since long passages make videos drag and voices can mispronounce names or slang; respelling words phonetically sometimes helps. Combine TTS with captions for accessibility. Creators often use TTS to narrate tips or stories without recording their own voice, which also helps when filming in noisy places.

Can Copilot do text to speech?

Yes. Microsoft Copilot can read its responses aloud in supported apps, and Copilot Voice lets you have spoken conversations, listening to your questions and replying with a natural voice.

In Microsoft 365 apps such as Word, the built-in Read Aloud feature reads documents, separate from Copilot itself, while Copilot can draft or summarise text that you then listen to. On Windows, Narrator provides system-wide screen reading. Availability of voice features depends on your app version, region and account type, so check the settings or speaker icons in your Copilot interface. For producing downloadable audio files, dedicated TTS services or Azure AI Speech, which powers many Microsoft voices, are better suited than chat assistants.

Why is text to speech helpful?

Text to speech makes written content accessible to people who are blind, have low vision, dyslexia or other reading difficulties, and lets anyone listen while commuting, cooking or exercising. It also helps with pronunciation, proofreading and focus.

Hearing your own writing read aloud exposes awkward sentences and typos you skim past. Language learners use TTS to hear correct pronunciation. People with lower literacy can access information in their own language, which matters greatly in multilingual countries such as India. KrishGyan, which won Changethon 2025, delivered farming advice to farmers in their own language over WhatsApp, because voice reaches people that text alone cannot. Businesses use TTS for voice assistants, phone systems and accessible products, and creators use it for narration.

Why is speech to text so bad sometimes?

Speech to text struggles with background noise, poor microphones, strong accents, fast or overlapping speakers, technical jargon, names and mixed-language speech such as Hinglish. Models trained mostly on certain accents and languages perform worse on others.

Accuracy has improved greatly with models such as Whisper and modern cloud services, but errors remain, especially without punctuation cues or with short, ambiguous phrases. Improve results with a good microphone close to the speaker, quiet rooms, clear pacing, and custom vocabulary or prompts listing names and terms. Choose services with strong support for your language. My OCR & Speech Workspace includes live speech-to-text, and real-world audio quality matters more than any single model setting. Always review transcripts before relying on them for important records.
