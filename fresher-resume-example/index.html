How should a fresher resume look? Example, headline, and no-internship tips

What a fresher resume should look like, a full example, the headline, building one with no internship, a CS and BCA template, length, and writing 'fresher'.

Key takeaways

  • A fresher resume is one clean page that leads with proof: projects with links, then skills, education and achievements.
  • Prepare it from a job description: pick the role, collect evidence, write bullets with results, then tailor per application.
  • With no internship, projects, freelance work, hackathons, open source and leadership roles fill the experience gap.
  • A headline states your target role and strongest skills in one line.
  • Do not write 'fresher' as a selling point; let the content show what you can do.

A fresher resume should be one clean, single-column page that proves you can do the job: a short headline and summary, two or three projects with links and results, grouped skills, education, and any internships, freelance work or achievements. Recruiters skim, so the strongest evidence goes first. This page walks through preparing it, the format, a computer science and BCA version with a full example, what to do with no internship, the headline, length, and whether to write "fresher".

How should a fresher resume look?

It should look simple and scannable: one page, one column, clear section headings, consistent fonts and spacing, and no photos, graphics or skill bars. Content should be specific, with links and numbers where possible.

Also asked as: how should be a fresher resume

A reader should understand in ten seconds what role you want, what you can do and where to see proof. That means a headline at the top, projects near the top for technical roles, and every bullet starting with an action verb and ending with a result. Use a standard template from Google Docs, Word or Overleaf, export as PDF, and check that text copies cleanly, because many companies screen resumes with software first.

How do I prepare a resume as a fresher?

Start from the job you want: collect three to five job descriptions for that role, list the skills they repeat, then gather your evidence for each skill from projects, courses, internships and activities, and write bullets that show it.

Also asked as: how to prepare a resume for fresher

Steps:

  • Choose one target role, such as junior backend developer.
  • List required skills from real job descriptions.
  • Collect your evidence: projects, coursework, freelance work, hackathons.
  • Write two or three bullets per item: action, tool, result.
  • Fit everything on one page; cut what does not support the role.
  • Tailor the summary and skills order for each application.
  • Ask a senior or mentor to review it.

Keep a longer master version and trim it per application.

What is the right resume format for a fresher?

Use a reverse-chronological, one-page format adapted for freshers: header, headline and summary, projects, skills, education, experience if any, and achievements. Put the most relevant section highest.

Also asked as: what is the format of resume for a fresher

For technical roles, projects come before education because they show ability; for roles where your degree is the main qualification, education can come first. Keep margins reasonable, fonts readable, and dates aligned. Avoid two-column templates, which often parse badly in applicant tracking systems. Name the file clearly, for example "FirstName-LastName-Resume.pdf". Use one date format throughout, such as "Jul 2025 to Dec 2025", aligned to the right so dates are easy to scan, and bold only headings and project names rather than random words.

How do I make a resume as a computer science fresher?

Lead with projects that match your target role, list technical skills grouped by type, include your GitHub and portfolio, and show problem-solving evidence such as competitive programming or hackathons.

Also asked as: how to make resume for computer science student fresher

What strong CS fresher resumes include:

  • Projects: two or three, deployed, with stack, what you built and a result.
  • Skills: languages, frameworks, databases, tools and cloud, only ones you can discuss.
  • Links: GitHub with clean READMEs, a portfolio, LinkedIn.
  • Education: degree, college, years, CGPA if it helps.
  • Achievements: hackathon results, coding contest ratings, open source.

For AI roles, one project with an LLM, retrieval or a fine-tuned model, documented with evaluation results, stands out.

How do I build a resume with no internship experience?

Replace internship experience with other evidence: personal and academic projects treated like work, freelance or volunteer work, hackathons, open-source contributions, teaching or tutoring, and leadership in clubs or communities.

Also asked as: how do you build a resume as a fresher with no internship experience

Make projects read like experience: "Built and deployed a document Q&A web app with FastAPI and an LLM API; 40 classmates used it during exams; answers cite page numbers." Freelance work for a local business, even small, shows client delivery. Hackathons show teamwork under pressure. Founding or organising a community shows initiative; I founded TechVerse Enclave, which grew to 500+ developers, and that kind of role is worth a line. Then apply for internships with this resume; one internship changes the next resume.

What is a resume headline for a fresher?

A resume headline is one line under your name that states your target role and strongest skills, such as "Fresher Python Developer | FastAPI, PostgreSQL, LLM APIs | 3 Deployed Projects". It tells recruiters instantly what you offer.

Also asked as: what is resume headline for fresher

Good headlines are specific and true. Examples:

  • "Junior Frontend Developer | React, Next.js, TypeScript | Hackathon Winner"
  • "Data Analyst | SQL, Python, Power BI | Analysed 3 Real Datasets End to End"
  • "GenAI Developer | Python, RAG, Vector Search | Built a Cited Document Assistant"

Match the headline to the job you are applying for. Job portals such as Naukri also have a separate headline field; use the same idea there.

What is an example of a fresher resume?

Here is a complete example in plain text form for a computer science or BCA fresher targeting backend or GenAI roles.

email@example.com | +91-XXXXXXXXXX | github.com/yourname | linkedin.com/in/yourname
Fresher Backend Developer | Python, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, LLM APIs

PROJECTS

Languages: Python, JavaScript, SQL | Frameworks: FastAPI, Next.js

EDUCATION

Replace every line with your own real details.

Where can I get a free BCA fresher resume template?

Use a free single-column template from Google Docs, Microsoft Word or Overleaf, and adapt the example above. BCA freshers need the same structure as other tech freshers: projects, skills, education and achievements.

Tips for BCA students: highlight projects and practical skills strongly, since recruiters may compare you with BTech candidates; mention your final-year project with a live link; and list any certifications that are directly relevant, such as a cloud fundamentals certification. Keep the design plain and the content specific. Avoid paid "premium" templates; content matters far more than design.

How many pages should a fresher resume be?

One page. A fresher rarely has enough relevant experience to fill two pages with strong content, and recruiters skim the first page in seconds.

To fit one page, cut school-level details, generic skills, long objectives, course lists and hobbies that add nothing. Keep two or three bullets per project. If you have research publications or several substantial internships, a second page may be justified, but the first page must still carry your best evidence. A tight one-page resume also signals that you can prioritise, which employers value.

Should I write "fresher" on my resume?

You can mention it in a headline for roles that explicitly hire freshers, such as "Fresher Python Developer", but do not make it your selling point. Let projects, skills and results show what you can do.

Also asked as: how to write fresher in resume

Avoid phrases like "I am a fresher looking for an opportunity to learn", which say nothing about what you offer. Instead write a summary like "Final-year BCA student who has built and deployed three Python web apps, including a cited document assistant used by classmates." On job portals, choose "fresher" or "0 years experience" where the form asks, and let your profile content do the rest.
