Key takeaways

  • Great Gemini prompts give a role, goal, context, constraints and output format.
  • Photo prompts work best when they describe style, setting, lighting and what must stay unchanged, like your face.
  • Only upload your own photos or photos you have consent to edit, and remember AI images are watermarked.
  • Jio users may get Google's AI Pro plan through partner offers; eligibility and duration depend on current terms.
  • Developers send parallel Gemini requests with async code, but must respect rate limits and use backoff.

The best Gemini prompts are specific: they tell Gemini who to act as, what you want, the context, any constraints, and the exact format of the answer, and photo prompts add style, setting, lighting and what must stay unchanged. Trending photo prompts for portraits and couples are popular in India, so they are covered here with privacy tips. I write prompts for AI products daily, so this page covers general and photo prompt examples, the Jio Gemini offer, Gemini courses, freezing fixes and parallel API requests.

What are the best Gemini prompts?

The best prompts include five parts: a role, a clear goal, relevant context, constraints, and the output format. "Explain photosynthesis" becomes "You are a biology tutor. Explain photosynthesis to a Class 10 student in five bullet points with one everyday example, then ask me one quiz question."

Also asked as: best gemini prompts for couple

High-value prompt patterns:

  • "Summarise this document in 7 bullets for a busy manager, with deadlines highlighted."
  • "Act as an interviewer for a fresher data analyst role; ask one question at a time."
  • "Rewrite this email to be polite and concise, under 120 words."
  • "Compare these two laptops for coding in a table with a recommendation."
  • "Create a 4-week study plan for DBMS with daily 1-hour sessions."

What are some example Gemini prompts?

Examples for everyday use: "Plan a three-day trip itinerary on a student budget with travel times", "Explain this error message and how to fix it", "Turn my meeting notes into action items with owners", and "Give me ten project ideas using RAG for college students".

For learning: "Teach me recursion with three examples from easy to hard, then test me." For writing: "Give me three headline options for this blog post in a professional tone." For coding: "Write a Python function that validates Indian phone numbers, with tests." Follow up to refine: ask for shorter answers, different tone, more examples or sources.

What are the best Gemini photo prompts for girls?

Popular photo-editing prompts transform a portrait into styles such as a vintage film look, a traditional saree portrait with golden-hour lighting, a professional studio headshot, or an artistic illustration. Always ask Gemini to keep the face and identity unchanged.

Also asked as: best gemini prompts for girls

Example prompts:

  • "Turn this into a retro 1990s film-style portrait with warm tones and soft grain; keep my face exactly the same."
  • "Create a studio portrait in a red saree with soft golden lighting and a plain backdrop; preserve facial features."
  • "Make a professional LinkedIn headshot with a neutral grey background and natural lighting."

Upload only your own photos, avoid sharing images of others without consent, and remember edited images carry AI watermarks.

What are the best Gemini photo prompts for boys?

Popular prompts for boys include cinematic portraits, streetwear fashion shoots, sports-poster styles, superhero-style illustrations and professional headshots, with instructions to keep the face and identity unchanged.

Also asked as: best gemini prompts for boys

Examples:

  • "Create a cinematic portrait at night in a city street with neon reflections; keep my face identical."
  • "Make a sports magazine cover style photo in a cricket jersey with stadium lights."
  • "Turn this into a clean professional headshot with a navy background and soft light."
  • "Create a 3D cartoon figurine version of me on a desk display."

Refine with follow-ups such as lighting, outfit colour or background, one change at a time.

What are the best Gemini photo prompts for men?

For men, effective prompts often target professional images, such as executive headshots and speaker portraits, plus stylised looks like vintage film, formal wedding portraits or outdoor adventure scenes, always preserving facial features.

Also asked as: best gemini prompts for men

Examples:

  • "Professional speaker portrait on a stage with soft spotlight and a blurred audience; same face and hairstyle."
  • "Executive headshot in a dark suit, light grey background, natural expression."
  • "Vintage black-and-white portrait with dramatic side lighting."

For professional use, such as LinkedIn or company profiles, choose natural results over heavy stylisation, and consider whether AI-edited photos are appropriate in your context.

What are the best Gemini photo prompts for couples?

Couple prompts often create romantic or festive portraits: golden-hour beach scenes, traditional wedding-style portraits, vintage film photos, or illustrated keepsakes, using a photo of both people and asking Gemini to keep both faces unchanged.

Examples:

  • "Turn this into a golden-hour portrait on a beach with soft warm light; keep both faces identical."
  • "Create a traditional Indian wedding-style portrait with marigold decorations."
  • "Make a vintage 1970s film photograph look with warm tones."
  • "Create a watercolour illustration of us for an anniversary card."

Get your partner's consent before uploading, and avoid uploading intimate photos to any AI service.

Which Gemini plan is free with Jio?

Reliance Jio and Google have offered eligible Jio users access to Google's AI Pro plan, which includes advanced Gemini features, for a limited period through partnership offers. Eligibility, duration and activation steps depend on the current offer terms.

Check the MyJio app or Jio's official website for the latest offer, eligible plans and age criteria, and activate it through official channels only. Beware of messages or websites claiming free Gemini access that ask for payments or passwords; they may be scams. Google also offers student promotions in some periods, worth checking on Google's official pages.

Is there a Gemini AI course?

Yes. Google offers free learning paths on Google Cloud Skills Boost and Google's AI learning pages covering Gemini, prompting and building with the Gemini API, and Kaggle has hosted Google's generative AI intensive courses with codelabs.

Also asked as: course gemini ai

A learning path: start with prompting basics and everyday Gemini use; then try Google AI Studio to experiment with models; then build a small app with the Gemini API, such as a document question-answering tool; and finally learn evaluation and safety. Courses help, but building one real project teaches the most.

Why does Gemini freeze up?

Gemini can freeze because of very long conversations, large uploads, heavy modes like Deep Research or image generation, slow networks, outdated apps or browsers, low device memory, or temporary service problems.

Fixes:

  • Start a new chat when one gets long.
  • Upload smaller files or fewer pages.
  • Update the app and browser; clear cache.
  • Close other heavy apps and tabs.
  • Switch networks or try mobile data.
  • Try the web version if the app freezes, or vice versa.

If it freezes for everyone at once, wait; service issues are usually resolved quickly.

How do I send parallel requests to the Gemini API?

Use asynchronous code to send several requests concurrently, for example with Python's asyncio and the Gemini SDK's async client, limiting concurrency with a semaphore so you stay within rate limits, and retrying 429 errors with exponential backoff.

Also asked as: how to send parallel request to google gemini

import asyncio
from google import genai
client = genai.Client()
sem = asyncio.Semaphore(5)
async def ask(prompt):
    async with sem:
        r = await client.aio.models.generate_content(model="gemini-2.5-flash", contents=prompt)
        return r.text
results = asyncio.run(asyncio.gather(*[ask(p) for p in ["Q1", "Q2", "Q3"]]))

For large offline jobs, use batch processing instead, which is cheaper and avoids rate-limit pressure.
