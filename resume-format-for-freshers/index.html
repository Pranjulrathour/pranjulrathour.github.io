Best resume format and templates for freshers, section by section

The best resume format for freshers: one page, projects first, and what to write in the summary, skills, profile and hobbies, plus Canva and CV vs resume.

Key takeaways

  • Use a simple one-column, one-page template that applicant tracking systems can read.
  • For tech freshers, order sections as header, summary, projects, skills, education, experience, achievements.
  • Write a two-line summary tailored to the role, and list skills you can prove in your projects.
  • Keep it to one page; a second page is rarely justified for a fresher.
  • Canva works if you pick a clean single-column template and export a text-based PDF.

The best resume format for freshers is a clean, one-page, single-column layout that puts your strongest evidence first: for tech roles, that means projects with links, then skills, education, internships and achievements. Simple templates beat designer ones because many companies screen resumes with applicant tracking systems that struggle with columns, tables and graphics. This page covers templates and format, CV versus resume, length, and what to write in the summary, profile, skills and hobbies sections, plus how to make a good resume in Canva.

What are the best resume templates for freshers?

The best templates are simple: one column, clear headings, standard fonts, and no photos, charts or skill bars. Google Docs and Word templates, LaTeX templates on Overleaf, and clean single-column Canva templates all work.

What to look for:

  • One column, so text reads in the right order for screening software.
  • Section headings such as Projects, Skills, Education, Experience.
  • Plenty of white space and consistent formatting.
  • Room for project links and short bullet points.

Avoid templates with two columns, icons for every skill, progress bars, photos or heavy colour. They look attractive but often parse badly and waste space. Recruiters spend seconds on a first read, so clarity wins.

What is the right resume format for freshers?

Use a reverse-chronological layout adapted for freshers: header, short summary, projects, skills, education, internships or experience, and achievements. Put the section that best proves your fit near the top.

Also asked as: what is resume format for freshers

For tech roles, a strong order is:

  • Header: name, phone, email, LinkedIn, GitHub, portfolio.
  • Summary: two lines tailored to the role.
  • Projects: two or three with stack, what you built, a result and a link.
  • Skills: grouped by languages, frameworks, tools, databases.
  • Education: degree, college, years, CGPA if strong.
  • Experience: internships, freelance work, leadership roles.
  • Achievements: hackathons, competitive programming, certifications that matter.

Use action verbs and real numbers where you have them.

Where can I get a free resume template for freshers?

Free sources include the resume templates in Google Docs and Microsoft Word, LaTeX templates on Overleaf, and free templates in Canva. Pick a single-column one and keep it plain.

Also asked as: free resume for freshers template

Google Docs is the quickest and exports clean PDFs. Overleaf templates produce neat, consistent resumes and are popular among engineering students; LaTeX takes an hour to learn. Canva is fine if you choose a minimal template and avoid graphic elements. Whatever you use, save the final version as a PDF, name it sensibly, such as "FirstName-LastName-Resume.pdf", and keep an editable copy so you can tailor it for each application.

Should a fresher use a CV or a resume?

In India, recruiters often use the words interchangeably and mean the same short document. Strictly, a resume is a one or two-page summary tailored to a job, while a CV is a longer, complete record of academic history, used for research, academia and some countries.

For freshers applying to companies, send a one-page resume, even if the application form says "CV". Use a long academic CV only for research positions, PhD or master's applications abroad, or when an institution specifically asks for full academic details such as publications, conferences and teaching. When applying internationally, check local conventions: in the UK and Europe, "CV" often means a two-page resume.

Can a fresher's resume be two pages?

Keep it to one page. A fresher rarely has enough relevant experience to fill two pages with strong content, and recruiters reading quickly may never reach page two.

Fit it on one page by cutting weak items: school-level achievements, generic skills like "MS Office" for a developer role, long objective statements, and course lists. Keep only projects and experience that support the role, with two or three bullet points each. If you have substantial research, publications or multiple internships, a second page can be justified, but then the first page must still carry your best evidence. Consistent, readable formatting matters more than squeezing in everything.

What is the summary in a resume for freshers?

The summary is a two to three-line introduction at the top that states your target role, your strongest skills and your best evidence. It replaces the old "objective" statement and should be tailored to each application.

Example for a GenAI role: "Final-year BCA student building GenAI applications with Python, FastAPI and LLM APIs. Built a document question-answering app with retrieval and citations, deployed and used by classmates. Looking for a GenAI engineering internship." Keep it specific and factual. Avoid clichés such as "hard-working team player seeking a challenging role". If you cannot write something specific, skip the summary and let projects speak.

What is the profile section in a resume for freshers?

A profile section is another name for the summary: a short paragraph about who you are professionally and what you offer. Some templates also use "profile" for links such as LinkedIn, GitHub and portfolio.

Also asked as: what is profile in resume for freshers

Write the profile in the same way as a summary: role, key skills, best evidence, in two or three lines. If your template has a separate profile links section, include LinkedIn, GitHub and a portfolio site, and make sure each link works and shows your best work. A GitHub with clean, pinned projects and good READMEs supports every claim in your resume, so prepare it before you send applications.

What should I write in the skills section of a fresher's resume?

List skills you can prove, grouped by type and matched to the job description: programming languages, frameworks, databases, tools and platforms. Every skill should appear in at least one project or experience entry.

An example for a developer:

  • Languages: Python, JavaScript, SQL.
  • Frameworks: FastAPI, React, Next.js.
  • Data and AI: pandas, scikit-learn, LLM APIs, embeddings and vector search.
  • Tools: Git, Docker, Linux, VS Code.
  • Cloud: Vercel, Render, basic AWS or Azure.

Avoid rating bars and percentages, which mean nothing to a reader. Skip skills you used once in a tutorial; interviewers will ask about anything listed.

Should I add hobbies to a fresher's resume?

Only if they add something: evidence of discipline, leadership, creativity or relevant skills, or a genuine conversation starter. If space is tight, drop hobbies before dropping projects.

Hobbies that help are specific: "run a 500-member student tech community", "competitive chess, state level", "write a weekly blog on web development", or "build robots for a school robotics club". Generic lines such as "reading, music, travelling" add little. Never list hobbies you cannot talk about, because interviewers sometimes use them to break the ice. For tech roles, community and building activities can sit under "Achievements" or "Leadership" instead.

How do I make a fresher's resume in Canva?

Pick a single-column, minimal template, replace the content with your own, remove decorative graphics, photos and skill bars, use standard fonts, and download it as a PDF. Then check that the text can be selected and copied in order.

Also asked as: how to make resume for freshers in canva

Steps:

  • Search Canva's resume templates and filter for simple, single-column designs.
  • Set the page to A4 and keep margins reasonable.
  • Use clear headings and bullet points, not text boxes scattered across the page.
  • Add clickable links to GitHub, LinkedIn and your portfolio.
  • Download as "PDF Standard" and test by copying all text into a plain text editor.

If the copied text comes out jumbled, an applicant tracking system may read it the same way, so simplify the layout.
