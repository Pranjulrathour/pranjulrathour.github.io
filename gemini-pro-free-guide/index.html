How to get Gemini Pro for free, and what Gemini Pro offers

Key takeaways

  • 'Gemini Pro' means two things: the Pro model, which free users can try within limits, and the Google AI Pro subscription.
  • Free routes are the free Gemini tier, Google AI Studio for developers, and time-limited student or telecom offers; check eligibility on Google's own pages.
  • Use Flash for quick everyday tasks and Pro or a thinking mode for hard maths, code and planning.
  • Daily limits differ by plan and change often; Google's Gemini Apps help page lists the current numbers.
  • Claim offers only through official apps such as MyJio or Google One, never through links in forwarded messages.

You can use Gemini Pro for free in three ways: the free Gemini app gives limited access to Google's Pro model, Google AI Studio lets developers try Pro models with free quotas, and Google has run time-limited offers that include the paid Google AI Pro plan at no cost for eligible students and for some telecom customers in India, such as Jio users. This page explains those routes, what the Pro plan adds, how Gemini, Flash, Pro and thinking modes differ, daily limits, family sharing, and how Gemini relates to Antigravity.

How can I get Gemini Pro for free?

Use the free Gemini app, which includes limited access to the Pro model, use Google AI Studio for free developer access, or claim an official offer that includes Google AI Pro at no cost if you are eligible.

Also asked as: how to gemini pro for free

The options in order of effort:

  • Free Gemini app: sign in at gemini.google.com or the mobile app; free accounts get some Pro model use with lower limits.
  • Google AI Studio: a developer playground where you can try current models, including Pro, with free usage quotas.
  • Official offers: Google has run student offers and telecom partnerships that include the paid plan for a limited period.

Offers change often, so confirm eligibility on Google's own pages. Avoid "free Gemini Pro" links shared in groups; they are a common route for phishing and account theft.

Is Gemini Pro free for students?

Google has offered its paid AI plan free for a limited period to students in several countries, including India, through verification with a college email or student ID. Whether an offer is open today depends on the country and date.

When an offer is live, you sign in with a personal Google account, verify student status through Google's verification partner, and the plan is applied for the stated period. It usually renews at the normal rate afterwards unless you cancel, so note the end date. Even without an offer, students can do a lot on the free tier: explaining concepts, debugging code, summarising lecture notes and planning projects. NotebookLM, also from Google, is free and very good for studying from your own notes and papers.

How do I get Gemini Pro with Jio?

Reliance Jio and Google announced a partnership in 2025 that gives eligible Jio users access to Google AI Pro at no cost for a limited period. Eligible users claim it from the MyJio app with the Google account they want to use.

Also asked as: how to gemini pro with jio

Open the MyJio app, look for the Google Gemini or Google AI Pro offer banner, check the eligibility conditions, which have depended on age group and plan, and follow the steps to link your Google account. The benefit then appears in the Gemini app and Google One. Rules, eligible plans and duration are set by Jio and Google and have changed since launch, so rely on the MyJio app and Google's announcement, not on screenshots shared online. Never enter OTPs or passwords on links that claim to unlock the offer.

What does Gemini Pro offer?

The Google AI Pro plan gives higher access to Google's most capable Gemini models, longer context for big files, Deep Research, video and image generation with higher limits, Gemini inside Gmail and Docs, NotebookLM with higher limits, and extra cloud storage.

For students and developers the useful parts are:

  • More use of the Pro model and thinking modes for hard maths and code.
  • Deep Research, which browses many sources and writes a cited report.
  • Uploading long PDFs and codebases thanks to a large context window.
  • Gemini in Gmail, Docs and Sheets for drafting and analysis.
  • Higher limits in Google's coding tools.

The exact feature list and limits change as Google updates the plan, so check the plan page on Google One. If you mostly ask quick questions, the free tier is enough.

What is the difference between Gemini and Gemini Pro?

Gemini is Google's family of AI models and the name of its assistant app. Gemini Pro refers either to the Pro model, the most capable tier in a Gemini generation, or to the Google AI Pro subscription that gives more access to it.

The model family has tiers: Flash-Lite for the cheapest, fastest tasks, Flash for most everyday work, and Pro for complex reasoning and coding. Some generations also add a deeper thinking mode on top. The Gemini app picks a model for you unless you choose one. The subscription changes your limits and features, not the model itself: a free user and a paid user asking the Pro model the same question get the same quality of answer, but the free user runs out sooner.

What is the difference between Gemini Flash and Pro?

Flash is the faster, cheaper model tuned for everyday tasks and high volume. Pro is the more capable model for complex reasoning, difficult code and long multi-step work, and it is slower and more expensive per request.

In practice:

  • Use Flash for summaries, emails, quick explanations, extraction from documents and chat features in your app.
  • Use Pro for debugging a tricky bug, designing a system, solving hard maths, or long analysis where a wrong step costs more than waiting.

For developers building with the Gemini API, Flash is usually the right default because latency and cost matter at scale. Start with Flash, measure where it fails, and route only those cases to Pro. That routing pattern is how most production AI apps keep costs under control.

When should I use Gemini Pro vs thinking mode?

Pro is a model tier; thinking is a way of running a model, where it reasons step by step before answering. Use a thinking mode when the task needs several steps of logic, and a standard fast mode when it does not.

Also asked as: when to use gemini pro vs thinking

Thinking helps with maths and physics problems, competitive programming, debugging, planning a project and comparing options with many constraints. It adds waiting time and uses more of your limits, so it is wasted on simple questions, rewriting text or quick facts. If an answer from the fast mode seems shallow or contradicts itself, rerun the same prompt with thinking on. For developers, the Gemini API exposes thinking controls on models that support it, so you can set how much reasoning to spend per request.

How many Gemini Pro queries do I get per day?

It depends on your plan: free accounts get a small daily allowance of Pro model prompts, and paid plans get much more. Google lists the current limits in the Gemini Apps help centre, and they change from time to time.

Also asked as: how many gemini pro queries per day

Limits are also counted per feature: Pro model prompts, Deep Research reports, image generation and video generation each have their own allowance. When you reach a limit, the app usually falls back to a faster model or asks you to wait. To stretch a free allowance, use Flash for routine tasks, write one clear, complete prompt instead of many short follow-ups, and save Pro and thinking for the problems that need them. Developers using the API have separate rate limits shown in Google AI Studio.

Can I share Gemini Pro with family?

Partly. Google AI Pro is a Google One plan, and its cloud storage can be shared with up to five family members in a Google One family group. Whether the Gemini features are shared too depends on the plan and country.

Set up a family group from your Google account, invite members, then enable sharing in Google One settings. Each member keeps their own private files and chats; sharing never exposes your Gemini conversations or Drive files to others. Google has been extending AI benefits to family members in some regions, so check the Google One help pages for your country before assuming everyone in the group gets Pro features. Offer-based plans, such as student or telecom offers, may have their own sharing rules.

Does Gemini Pro include Antigravity?

Antigravity is Google's agent-first coding environment and is offered separately from the Gemini app. Individuals have been able to use it without a paid plan within rate limits, and Google AI subscriptions have come with higher limits.

Antigravity runs coding agents that plan and carry out tasks across an editor, a terminal and a browser, using Gemini models and some models from other providers. Because it is new, its pricing, limits and the benefits attached to Google AI plans have changed several times, so check Google's Antigravity site and the plan page before deciding. For learning to code with AI, the free options, such as Antigravity's free limits, Gemini in the browser, or Gemini Code Assist's free tier for individuals, are usually enough.
