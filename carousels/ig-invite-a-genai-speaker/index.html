Invite a GenAI speaker: the checklist

8 slidesPosted on Instagram · Threads · Facebook · LinkedIn · Bluesky · Devto

How to invite a GenAI speaker to your college — the whole checklist. The email is two lines: two dates + your students' year. Pick a talk (RAG, fine-tuning on a free GPU, shipping AI as a small team, what wins a hackathon, BCA to GenAI engineer), a 90-minute workshop, or a judging seat. The AV list: HDMI tested the day before, power for laptops, Wi-Fi for 60, one coordinator who replies. Free for student communities. Previous: VSICS Kanpur (talk show, mentorship session, BCA induction for 100+), IIT Roorkee, CSJMU, MeetKats.
