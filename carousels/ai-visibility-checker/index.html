Can ChatGPT even see your website? The AI Visibility Checker I built

10 slidesPosted on LinkedIn

Most websites block AI crawlers without knowing it. One robots.txt line written for Google in 2019 quietly keeps GPTBot, ClaudeBot and PerplexityBot out — and then the owner wonders why ChatGPT never mentions them. So I built a checker: paste a URL, get a 0–100 AI-visibility score and the exact fixes. Ten crawlers, five scored signals, no signup, nothing stored. Here's what it checks, how the score is built, and what it doesn't promise. Free at tools.scult.in. Built entirely solo and free for users, forever. If you're a company like Sarvam AI, Semrush, Cloudflare that acquires or invests in tools like this, I'd genuinely love to talk — pranjulrathour41@gmail.com.
