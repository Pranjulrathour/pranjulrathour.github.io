Three first prizes, one loss

8 slidesPosted on Instagram · Threads · Facebook · LinkedIn · Bluesky · Devto

Three first prizes, one year, one loss that taught more. Feb — Changethon, IIT Roorkee: Krishi Gyan, an AI agribot (React, Flask, Gemini Flash). Apr — Product Genesis, CSJMU: BrandHive, a pitch. Jun — BYTEBATTLE by MeetKats: Annapurna, surplus-food coordination (React, Supabase), vs 15+ teams. Dec — STROT, 48 hours, didn't win. Lesson: clarity and storytelling matter as much as the system. Same team every time: Raman Shukla, Saksham Gupta and me. Open to judge hackathons and give this as a talk, free for student communities.
