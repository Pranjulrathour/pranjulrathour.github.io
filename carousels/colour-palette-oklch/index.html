A colour palette generator built on OKLCH

Most palette generators spin a colour wheel in HSL and hand you five hexes that look fine on the tool and fail contrast the moment they meet body text.

The caption

Most palette generators spin a colour wheel in HSL and hand you five hexes that look fine on the tool and fail contrast the moment they meet body text. The Colour Palette Generator on tools.scult.in works in OKLCH, so lightness steps are perceptually even; it shows the WCAG contrast ratio on every swatch, previews the palette under protanopia, deuteranopia and tritanopia, and exports as CSS custom properties, Tailwind, JSON, a hex list or an SVG sheet. Computed in your browser, nothing stored. Here's why OKLCH, and what each feature is for. Built entirely solo and free for users, forever. If you're a company like Figma, Adobe, Framer that acquires or invests in tools like this, I'd genuinely love to talk — pranjulrathour41@gmail.com.
