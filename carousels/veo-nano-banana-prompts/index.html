34 Veo and 30 Nano Banana prompts

9 slides

A Veo prompt that reads like a sentence is a lottery ticket. The 34 Veo prompts on tools.scult.in are structured as layered briefs instead — subject and action, one camera move, environment, style, native audio — in the fixed order the model responds to. Product hero clips, vertical hook clips, lip-synced dialogue, voiceover explainers, property walkthroughs, fashion lookbooks. The 30 Nano Banana prompts do the same for Gemini's image model: catalog-ready product photos, hex-palette mood boards, one surgical edit that touches nothing else, a consistent character across scenes, virtual try-on, a pure-white marketplace background that meets Amazon's spec. Every prompt lists the exact tool and version it was tested against — Veo 3.1, Nano Banana (Gemini 3.1 Flash Image) — with the date. Try it free: https://tools.scult.in/prompts/veo Built entirely solo and free for users, forever.
