FAQ Schema Generator

8 slides

The rule that gets FAQ markup penalised: the questions and answers in your JSON-LD must appear on the page as real, visible text — the same words. A tidy FAQPage block with no matching on-page copy violates Google's guidelines and is treated as a manual-action risk. The FAQ Schema Generator on tools.scult.in satisfies that rule by construction: Q&A in, valid FAQPage JSON-LD out, plus a paste-ready visible HTML block. It flags duplicate questions, empty answers and answers Google would truncate, and it never uploads what you type. Slides: the visibility rule, what the tool flags, and why the schema.org validator is now the one to use for FAQ.
