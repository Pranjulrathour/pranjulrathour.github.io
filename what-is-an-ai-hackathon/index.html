What is an AI hackathon? Rules, project ideas and tools, from a winner

An AI hackathon is a short build sprint around AI. What GenAI and agentic AI hackathons involve, whether AI tools are allowed, and the projects that win.

An AI hackathon is a time-boxed competition, usually 24 to 48 hours, where teams build a working prototype that uses artificial intelligence to solve a problem, then present it to judges. The theme might be generative AI, machine learning or agents, and the best projects pair a real user problem with a demo that works. I have won three hackathon first prizes and judged and mentored others, so this page draws on both sides of the table: what each kind of AI hackathon is, whether AI tools are allowed, the projects and tools that win, and the big hackathons to aim for.

What is an AI hackathon?

An AI hackathon is an event where teams of two to five people build, in a fixed time, a prototype that uses AI to solve a problem, and pitch it with a live demo. Judges score the problem, the solution, technical depth and the presentation.

Also asked as: what is ai hackathon · what is gen ai hackathon

Hackathons run in colleges, at companies and online, often with themes such as healthcare, agriculture, education or sustainability. You usually get a problem statement or an open theme, mentors to unblock you, and food and sleep in short supply. The output is not a finished product; it is a convincing proof that an idea works. KrishGyan, which won Changethon 2025 at IIT Roorkee, was exactly that: farming advice delivered in the farmer's own voice and language over WhatsApp, demonstrated live.

What is a GenAI hackathon?

A GenAI hackathon focuses on generative AI: projects built with large language models, image, audio or video generation. Typical builds are chat assistants over documents, content tools, voice interfaces and copilots for a specific job.

Organisers often provide API credits from model providers and judge how well teams go beyond a plain chatbot. Strong GenAI projects ground the model in real data with retrieval, use structured outputs so results can drive actions, and handle failure honestly, for example by refusing to answer when evidence is weak. Judges have seen many "chat with your PDF" demos, so a clear user, a specific workflow and a measurable result stand out more than the model you used.

What is an AI/ML hackathon?

An AI/ML hackathon covers both generative AI and classic machine learning: prediction, classification, recommendation, computer vision and analytics. Some are data challenges where you train models on a provided dataset and are scored on accuracy.

Also asked as: what is ai ml hackathon

There are two formats. In build hackathons, you create an application and the model is one part of it. In data challenges, closer to Kaggle competitions, a leaderboard ranks your predictions on hidden test data. For build formats, use pre-trained models and focus on the product. For data challenges, invest in clean features, solid validation and a few well-tuned models rather than exotic architectures. Read which format you are entering before the event, because the winning strategies are completely different.

What is an agentic AI hackathon?

An agentic AI hackathon asks teams to build AI agents: systems that plan and take multi-step actions with tools, such as searching, calling APIs, filling forms or writing code, rather than only answering questions.

Also asked as: what is agentic ai hackathon

Judges look for agents that complete a real task end to end, show their steps, and stay safe: asking for confirmation before irreversible actions, staying within permissions, and recovering from errors. Good agent projects keep scope narrow, for example "an agent that books a lab slot and emails the confirmation", and give the agent a few well-described tools. Standards such as the Model Context Protocol make it quicker to connect tools. Log every step the agent takes; a visible trace makes the demo more convincing and helps you debug at 3 a.m.

Is AI allowed in hackathons?

Usually yes. Most hackathons now allow AI coding assistants and AI APIs, but some restrict pre-written code, require code written during the event, or ban specific tools. Always read the rules and ask organisers when unsure.

Even where AI is allowed, judges test understanding. Expect questions such as "why did you choose this approach?" or "what happens if the model returns an empty answer?". As a judge, I trust teams that can explain their architecture and trade-offs, and I am wary of polished demos the team cannot explain. Use AI tools to move faster, keep a Git history that shows your work during the event, and disclose generated assets where the rules ask for it.

What are the best AI hackathon projects?

The best AI hackathon projects solve a specific problem for a specific person, use AI where it adds something people cannot easily do, and work live in front of judges. Social impact and a clear user story score well.

Patterns that win:

  • Voice and local language access: like KrishGyan, AI that serves people who cannot use English apps.
  • Coordination problems: like Annapurna, which won BYTEBATTLE by matching surplus food with people who need it.
  • Document automation: extracting fields from forms, invoices or certificates with validation.
  • Grounded assistants: answers from a trusted source, with citations, for a college, clinic or government scheme.
  • Accessibility: tools for people with visual, hearing or learning difficulties.

Pick one user, one painful task and one wow moment in the demo.

What are the best AI tools for a hackathon?

Use hosted model APIs, a quick front end, a simple backend and free deployment. Training models during a hackathon rarely pays off; integrating good ones well does.

Also asked as: best ai tools for hackathon

A practical stack:

  • Models: an API from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google or Groq for fast inference; open models through Hugging Face when needed.
  • Speech: speech-to-text and text-to-speech APIs for voice features.
  • Retrieval: a small vector index or pgvector for document Q&A.
  • Backend: FastAPI or Node.js.
  • Front end: Next.js, React or Streamlit for the fastest UI.
  • Deployment: free tiers on Vercel, Render or Hugging Face Spaces.
  • Coding help: an AI coding assistant, used with care.

Set up keys, a repository and a deployed "hello world" before the clock starts if the rules allow it.

What do you see at an AI hackathon?

You see teams split between ambitious ideas and working demos, last-minute integration problems, and a final hour where most of the outcome is decided by who has a stable demo and a clear story.

Also asked as: what i saw at an ai hackathon

From the judge's table, the patterns repeat. Many teams build similar chatbots; the ones that stand out have spoken to a real user, narrowed the problem and show one feature working end to end. Live demos fail more often from network and API key problems than from bad ideas, so the winners rehearse and keep a recorded backup. Mentors are underused: ten minutes with one early can save hours. And the best teams stop adding features a few hours before the end to polish the demo and the pitch.

What is India's biggest AI hackathon?

There is no single official title, but Smart India Hackathon, run with the Ministry of Education's Innovation Cell, is among the largest hackathons in India, and many of its problem statements involve AI and machine learning.

Beyond it, large AI-themed hackathons in India are run by technology companies, cloud providers and startups, and listed on platforms such as Devfolio and Unstop, along with college tech festivals at IITs, NITs and other institutes. Sizes and themes change every year, so check the current season's listings. For a student, the "biggest" hackathon matters less than a well-run one in your area with good mentors, where you can finish a project and meet people.

What is a global AI hackathon?

A global AI hackathon is an online or multi-city event open to participants from many countries, often run by AI companies, cloud providers or communities, with remote judging and prizes such as cash, credits or incubation support.

Also asked as: what is global ai hackathon

They are a good option if there are few events near you: you can join from anywhere, team up with people from other countries and get feedback from international judges. The trade-offs are time zones, less mentoring and a very large number of submissions, so a short, sharp demo video and a clear README matter more than at in-person events. Find them on Devfolio, Devpost, Major League Hacking and the developer pages of AI companies. Treat each one as a deadline to ship a portfolio project.
