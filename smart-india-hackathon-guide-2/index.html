What is Smart India Hackathon? Who runs it, how it works, eligibility and SPOC

Smart India Hackathon explained: who runs it, when it began, how rounds work, eligibility, SPOC, problem statements, presenting and results.

Key takeaways

  • It is run by the Ministry of Education's Innovation Cell with AICTE and partners, and began in 2017.
  • Teams register through their institution's SPOC, compete in an internal hackathon, and shortlisted teams reach the grand finale.
  • Choose a problem statement you understand, can get data for, and can demonstrate convincingly.

Smart India Hackathon (SIH) is a nationwide initiative in which student teams from colleges across India build solutions to problem statements posed by central ministries, state departments, public sector bodies and industry, with software and hardware editions and a grand finale for shortlisted teams. It is among the largest hackathons in the country. As a three-time hackathon winner who has judged and mentored teams, I have put together the essentials: who runs SIH and when it began, how it works, eligibility, the SPOC and internal hackathon, problem statements, presenting, and checking results.

What is Smart India Hackathon?

Smart India Hackathon is a national programme that gives students the chance to solve real problems faced by government and industry, by building working prototypes and presenting them to experts. It runs in editions, typically with a software category and a hardware category.

Also asked as: what is sih smart india hackathon · when smart india hackathon started

Problem statements are published on the official portal, and teams choose one, propose a solution, and, if selected, build it during a grand finale at nodal centres. Beyond prizes, SIH offers exposure to real problems, mentoring from domain experts, and a chance for strong solutions to be taken forward. For students, it is a major line on a CV and an intense learning experience in teamwork and building under pressure.

Who conducts Smart India Hackathon?

Smart India Hackathon is conducted by the Ministry of Education's Innovation Cell together with AICTE, with support from partner organisations, ministries, state governments and companies that contribute problem statements, mentors and judges.

The organisers manage registration, problem statements, evaluation and the grand finale, while participating institutions coordinate their teams through a designated SPOC. Problem statement owners, such as ministries or companies, often take part in evaluating solutions for their statements. Official announcements, rules and results are published on the SIH website and official social channels.

When did Smart India Hackathon start?

Smart India Hackathon began in 2017 and has been held in editions since then, growing in the number of participating students, institutions and problem statements, with additional formats such as a junior edition for school students in some years.

Each edition updates its rules, timelines and categories, so information from previous years may not apply. If you are planning ahead, read past editions' problem statements to understand the style and domains, such as agriculture, health, education, smart automation, disaster management and sustainability, then check the current edition's official guidelines for dates and eligibility.

How does Smart India Hackathon work?

The typical flow is: problem statements are published, institutions run internal hackathons to select teams, SPOCs nominate selected teams with idea presentations on the portal, experts evaluate submissions, and shortlisted teams build their solutions at the grand finale, where winners are chosen per problem statement.

Stages in brief:

  • Problem statements: published on the official portal.
  • Internal hackathon: each institution selects its best teams.
  • Idea submission: teams submit a presentation in the official format through the SPOC.
  • Evaluation: experts shortlist teams for each statement.
  • Grand finale: teams build and present at nodal centres, often over a continuous multi-hour hackathon with mentoring rounds.

Exact steps change by edition; follow the current guidelines.

Who is eligible for Smart India Hackathon?

Students currently enrolled in recognised higher education institutions in India are eligible, participating in teams through their institution. Rules on team size and composition, such as including at least one female member, are set for each edition.

Also asked as: who are eligible for smart india hackathon

Typical requirements cover team size, mixing members across branches or years, a mentor from the institution, and participation through the institution's SPOC rather than directly. Check the current edition's eligibility rules on the official website, since they can change. If your college has not registered, ask your faculty or innovation cell coordinator to register and appoint a SPOC.

What is a SPOC in Smart India Hackathon?

SPOC means Single Point of Contact: a faculty member or official designated by the institution to manage SIH participation, including registration, the internal hackathon, nominating teams and communicating with organisers.

Also asked as: what is spoc in smart india hackathon

Students should contact their SPOC early to learn internal deadlines, the selection process and how to submit ideas. The SPOC uploads selected teams' details and presentations on the portal and receives official communication. If you do not know who your SPOC is, ask your department head, training and placement cell or institution's innovation council. Respect internal deadlines; they are usually earlier than the national ones.

What is the internal Smart India Hackathon?

The internal hackathon is the college-level round where an institution evaluates its student teams and selects the best ideas to nominate for the national stage. Each institution runs it in its own format, guided by the official instructions.

Also asked as: what is internal smart india hackathon

It is your first filter, so treat it seriously: choose a problem statement, research it, build an early prototype if possible, and present clearly using the official template. Judges at internal rounds look for understanding of the problem, feasibility, innovation and team capability. Feedback from this round is valuable; use it to sharpen your idea before national submission.

What are the Smart India Hackathon problem statements?

Problem statements are real challenges published by ministries, departments, public sector organisations and companies, each describing a problem, context and expected outcome, across themes such as agriculture, healthcare, education, disaster management, clean energy, transport and smart automation.

Also asked as: what is smart india hackathon 2025 problem statements

To choose well:

  • Read the full statement, including organisation and category.
  • Prefer statements where you understand the user and can access or simulate data.
  • Check how many teams might pick it; popular statements need a sharper angle.
  • Sketch a demo you could build in the finale time.

Use the official portal for the current edition's list; unofficial lists can be outdated or incomplete.

How do I present for Smart India Hackathon?

Use the official presentation template exactly, and make every slide count: problem understanding, proposed solution, technical approach, feasibility, impact and the team. Include diagrams, mock-ups or prototype screenshots instead of long text.

Also asked as: how to present smart india hackathon

Tips from judging experience:

  • Open with the user and the problem in one clear slide.
  • Show the solution flow as a diagram.
  • Name the technologies and explain why they fit.
  • Address risks: data availability, scalability, cost.
  • Show impact with realistic, sourced numbers where possible.
  • Rehearse answers to likely questions.

At the finale, a working demo of the core flow outweighs ambitious slides.

How do I check Smart India Hackathon results?

Check results on the official SIH website and official social media channels, where shortlisted teams and winners are announced for each edition. Your SPOC also receives communication about your team's status.

Also asked as: how to check smart india hackathon results

Be cautious of unofficial websites and forwarded messages claiming results or asking for payments to "confirm selection"; SIH does not charge teams to participate. If results are delayed, follow official channels for updates. Whatever the outcome, keep your project: a well-documented SIH prototype with a clear README is a strong portfolio item and a good story for interviews.
