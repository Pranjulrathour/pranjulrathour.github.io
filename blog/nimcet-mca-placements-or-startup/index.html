NIMCET, placements, or a startup: choosing your Year 3

The three real paths a final-year engineering student has, and an honest framework for choosing between them instead of drifting into one.

By Year 3, most engineering students are quietly panicking about three paths at once: higher studies through NIMCET or MCA, campus placements, or trying to build something of their own. Splitting focus across all three usually means being mediocre at each.

NIMCET / MCA: go all in, or don't go at all

This path rewards depth in a narrow set of topics over a fixed timeline. Half-preparing for an entrance exam while also job-hunting is the worst version of both strategies.

Placements: skills plus proof, not just a resume line

A portfolio with real, deployed projects changes a placement interview from a quiz about theory into a conversation about decisions you actually made. That conversation is easier to win.

Startup: build the MVP, form the team, before you decide

I co-founded SCULT INDIA out of exactly this path, alongside teammates I'd already built with in hackathons. The team and the MVP should exist before the decision to go all-in does, not after.

The one rule that applies to all three

Whichever you choose, mentor a junior along the way. It's the step everyone skips and the one that compounds the most — it completes the circle instead of leaving it open.
