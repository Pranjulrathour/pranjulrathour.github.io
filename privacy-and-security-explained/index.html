What is privacy and security? The difference, and what it means for AI

Privacy decides who may use your data and how; security protects it. The difference, why both matter, and how they apply to AI, phones and social media.

Key takeaways

  • Privacy decides who may collect and use personal data and for what; security is the set of controls that stops anyone else getting to it.
  • You can have security without privacy, but not privacy without security.
  • In AI systems, collect less, store derived data such as embeddings instead of raw images where possible, and keep personal data out of prompts and logs.
  • Models can leak training data through inference attacks; differential privacy and data minimisation are the main defences.
  • In India the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 sets the rules for personal data; HIPAA is the US rule set for health data.

Privacy is about who is allowed to collect, see and use information about a person, and for what purpose. Security is the set of technical and organisational controls that keep information safe from people who are not allowed. They overlap but are not the same: a system can be very secure and still misuse your data. This page explains the difference, why both matter, what they mean for data, apps, social media and iPhones, how they apply to AI systems including inference attacks, and what the HIPAA privacy and security rules cover.

What is privacy and security?

Privacy is a person's right to control how information about them is collected, used and shared. Security is the protection of information and systems against unauthorised access, change or loss.

Put simply, privacy answers "should this data be collected and used this way?", and security answers "is this data protected from those who should not have it?". A privacy decision is limiting a sign-up form to the fields you actually need. A security control is encrypting the database that stores those fields and requiring two-factor authentication for the admins. Good systems design both together: collect less, protect what you keep, and delete it when the purpose is over.

Do privacy and security mean the same thing?

No. Security is about protection; privacy is about appropriate use. You can have security without privacy, but you cannot have privacy without security.

Two examples make the difference clear. A company that encrypts all its data perfectly but sells your browsing history to advertisers has strong security and poor privacy. A company with a strong privacy policy that leaves its database open on the internet has good intentions and no real privacy, because anyone can read the data. Security is necessary for privacy but not enough. Privacy adds questions security does not ask: do you have consent, is this the minimum data needed, how long will you keep it, and can the person see or delete it?

Why is privacy and security important?

They protect people from harm such as fraud, identity theft, stalking, discrimination and embarrassment, and they protect organisations from breaches, fines and lost trust. Laws in most countries now require both.

For individuals, leaked phone numbers, ID details and passwords lead directly to scams and account takeovers. For organisations, a single breach can mean regulatory penalties, legal claims and customers who never return. For engineers, privacy and security are also design quality: systems that collect less data are simpler to secure and cheaper to run. In India, the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 sets duties for anyone processing personal data, so these are now legal requirements, not just good practice.

What is data privacy and security?

Data privacy is the set of rules and practices for handling personal data properly: consent, purpose, minimisation, retention and individual rights. Data security is the protection of that data through encryption, access control, monitoring and backups.

A practical checklist for any app that stores user data:

  • Collect only the fields you need and record why you need each one.
  • Encrypt data in transit with TLS and at rest in the database.
  • Give each person and service the least access they need.
  • Log access to sensitive data and review the logs.
  • Set retention periods and actually delete old data.
  • Let users see, correct and delete their data.

Most data breaches come from ordinary failures: weak passwords, exposed storage buckets and unpatched software.

What is digital privacy and security?

Digital privacy and security is the same idea applied to your online life: controlling what your devices, apps and accounts reveal about you, and protecting them from being hacked or misused.

The basics give most of the protection. Use a password manager and unique passwords, turn on two-factor authentication for email and banking, keep your phone and laptop updated, and install apps only from official stores. Review app permissions for location, contacts, camera and microphone, and remove the ones an app does not need. Be careful with public Wi-Fi for sensitive accounts, and treat unexpected links in messages as suspicious, because phishing is still the most common way accounts are lost. Your email account is the master key to the rest, so protect it most.

What is privacy and security in AI?

In AI, privacy means controlling what personal data goes into training, prompts, logs and outputs. Security means protecting models and AI apps from attacks such as prompt injection, data leakage and model theft.

AI adds new risks to old ones. Models can memorise and repeat training data. Users paste personal or confidential information into prompts that may be logged. Prompt injection can trick an AI app into revealing data or taking actions it should not. Good practice is to minimise personal data, mask it before it reaches a model, restrict what tools an AI agent can call, and review the OWASP list of risks for LLM applications. In FaceVision I designed for this: recognition runs in the browser and the server stores face embeddings only, never images.

What is a privacy inference attack in AI security?

A privacy inference attack tries to learn private information from a trained model's outputs, for example whether a specific person's record was in the training data, or a sensitive attribute about them.

Also asked as: what is privacy inference in ai security

The main types:

  • Membership inference: deciding whether a given record was used in training, often because models are more confident on data they have seen.
  • Attribute inference: predicting a sensitive attribute, such as a health condition, from other known features.
  • Model inversion and data extraction: reconstructing training examples, or getting a language model to repeat memorised text.

Defences include differential privacy during training, which adds calibrated noise so no single record changes the model much, limiting how much detail outputs reveal, removing duplicates and personal data from training sets, and testing models with these attacks before release.

What is privacy and security in online social media?

On social media, privacy is about who can see your posts, profile and contacts, and what the platform does with your data. Security is about protecting your account from takeover and your information from scraping and scams.

Steps worth taking on every platform: review who can see your posts and contact you, turn off location tagging, hide your phone number and email from your profile, and enable two-factor authentication. Be careful what you share: your college, daily routine and photos of documents give attackers more than you think. Check which third-party apps are connected to your account and remove old ones. Remember that "private" posts can still be screenshotted, and that the platform itself sees everything you post.

What is privacy and security on iPhone?

On iPhone, Privacy & Security is the section of Settings where you control which apps can access your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone and other data, and where security features such as Safety Check and Lockdown Mode live.

Useful settings to review:

  • Location Services: set apps to "While Using" or "Never", and turn off precise location where it is not needed.
  • Tracking: refuse app tracking requests so apps cannot follow you across other apps.
  • Photos and contacts: give limited access instead of full access.
  • Safety Check: review and reset who has access to your information.
  • Lockdown Mode: an extreme protection mode for people facing targeted attacks.

Keep iOS updated, because many updates fix actively exploited security flaws.

What are the HIPAA privacy and security rules?

HIPAA is a United States law for health information. Its Privacy Rule sets when protected health information may be used or disclosed; its Security Rule sets the safeguards required to protect electronic health information.

Also asked as: what is hipaa privacy and security rules

The Privacy Rule covers patients' rights, such as access to their records, and limits on how hospitals, insurers and their partners share data. The Security Rule requires administrative, physical and technical safeguards: risk analysis, access controls, audit logs, encryption where appropriate and staff training. A third piece, the Breach Notification Rule, requires notice when protected data is exposed. If you build a health app for US clients you will meet HIPAA. In India, health data falls under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and sector rules, so check the requirements for each market you serve.
