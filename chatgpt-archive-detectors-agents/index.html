Key takeaways

  • Archived ChatGPT chats are found in Settings under archived chats, where you can view, unarchive or delete them.
  • Third-party ChatGPT sites call OpenAI's API or other models; use the official site for privacy and reliability.
  • AI text detectors guess using statistical patterns and are unreliable; never treat them as proof.
  • ChatGPT works by predicting tokens with a transformer trained on vast text and refined with human feedback.
  • Free courses with certificates come from platforms like Microsoft Learn and Google; Hindi tutorials are widely available.

Archived ChatGPT chats are kept in your settings: open Settings, find the archived chats option, and select Manage to view, unarchive or delete them. Archiving hides chats from the sidebar without deleting them. I build AI products and teach students to use AI tools responsibly, so this page covers the archive, third-party ChatGPT sites, how AI detectors really work, how ChatGPT works technically, work versus personal use, ChatGPT agents and building them, and free courses including Hindi resources.

Where is the ChatGPT archive?

In ChatGPT, open Settings, go to the General or Data controls section depending on your app version, and choose Archived chats, then Manage. You will see all archived conversations and can open, unarchive or delete them.

Also asked as: where chatgpt archive

To archive a chat, open its menu in the sidebar and choose Archive. Archived chats keep their content and can be found through search in some versions. Archiving is useful for decluttering without losing history. If you want chats gone permanently, delete them instead. Settings locations change across app updates, so check OpenAI's help article if menus look different. For long-term records, export your data from data controls, which emails you an archive of your conversations, useful before deleting old chats.

How do ChatGPT sites work?

Third-party "ChatGPT" websites and apps usually send your messages to OpenAI's API or other model providers, then show the responses in their own interface, sometimes adding features such as templates, document tools or extra models.

Some are legitimate products built on AI APIs; others are low-quality or scams that collect personal data, charge for access to free features, or show excessive ads. The official ChatGPT is at chatgpt.com and in OpenAI's official apps. Before using third-party sites, check who runs them, their privacy policy, and whether you really need them. Never enter passwords or sensitive data into unknown AI sites.

How do ChatGPT detectors work?

AI text detectors estimate whether text was machine-written by looking at statistical patterns, such as how predictable each word is to a language model, often called perplexity, and how much sentence structure varies, sometimes called burstiness, or by using classifiers trained on human and AI text.

They are unreliable: they produce false positives, flagging human writing, especially by non-native English writers, and false negatives when AI text is edited or paraphrased. OpenAI withdrew its own AI text classifier in 2023 because of low accuracy. Detectors should never be used as sole proof of misconduct. Better approaches for educators include process-based assessment, drafts, oral explanations and clear AI-use policies.

How does ChatGPT work technically?

Technically, ChatGPT is a transformer neural network that converts text into tokens and predicts the next token based on all previous tokens in the context, repeating to generate responses. Attention layers let it weigh relationships between all parts of the input.

It is pretrained on huge text datasets to learn language and knowledge, then fine-tuned with supervised examples and reinforcement learning from human feedback to follow instructions. At inference, it runs on GPU clusters, may call tools such as web search or code execution, and streams tokens back to you. Reasoning models add internal step-by-step thinking before answering.

What is ChatGPT doing, and why does it work?

"What Is ChatGPT Doing and Why Does It Work?" is a widely read 2023 essay by Stephen Wolfram explaining that ChatGPT generates text one word at a time by predicting likely continuations, and that training on huge amounts of text captures much of the structure of language and knowledge.

The essay explains embeddings, neural networks and training in accessible terms, and argues that language has more regularity than we assumed, which is why prediction produces coherent text. It remains a good conceptual introduction, though models have since added tools, reasoning modes and multimodality. Pair it with hands-on experiments to build intuition, such as comparing answers at different temperature settings through the API, which shows the probabilistic nature of generation directly.

When should I use ChatGPT for work vs a personal chat?

Use a work or business ChatGPT workspace, provided by your organisation, for company tasks and information, since business plans offer admin controls and data protections. Use personal accounts for personal tasks, and avoid putting confidential work data into personal accounts.

Also asked as: when to use chatgpt work vs chat

Organisations often set policies on which AI tools are approved and what data can be shared. Business workspaces may connect to company apps and keep work history separate from personal history. If your organisation does not provide an approved tool, ask before using personal AI accounts for work, especially with client data, code or personal information about others.

What are ChatGPT agents?

ChatGPT agents are features that let ChatGPT carry out multi-step tasks for you, using tools such as a virtual browser, code execution and connected apps, rather than only answering. Agent mode can research, compare options, fill forms and create files, asking permission before important actions.

Custom GPTs are related but simpler: specialised assistants with instructions, knowledge files and optional actions that call APIs. Agents work best on clear, bounded tasks with checkable results. Supervise them, review outputs, and be careful granting access to accounts or payments, since agents can make mistakes or be misled by malicious web content.

How do I create AI agents using ChatGPT?

For no-code assistants, create a custom GPT with instructions, knowledge files and actions connecting to APIs through OpenAPI schemas. For developer-built agents, use OpenAI's APIs and agent tools to define tools, run the agent loop and add guardrails.

Also asked as: how to create ai agents using chatgpt

Steps for a custom GPT: describe its role and tasks, upload reference documents, add actions for APIs it should call, test with real requests, and refine instructions. For developer agents: define narrow tools with clear schemas, cap steps, log actions, require approval for consequential steps, and evaluate on real tasks. Start with one narrow job before expanding.

Is there a free ChatGPT course with a certificate?

Several free courses offer certificates or badges: Microsoft Learn's generative AI paths, Google's AI essentials resources at times, and platforms such as Coursera and edX where auditing is free and certificates may be paid or available through financial aid.

Also asked as: chatgpt course free with certificate

OpenAI also publishes learning resources and guides. Check each course's current terms, since free certificate offers change. Certificates help a little; employers value practical skills more: show how you used ChatGPT to complete real projects, such as research reports, automations or a small app, with documented results.

Is there a free ChatGPT course in Hindi?

Yes. Many educators publish free ChatGPT tutorials in Hindi on YouTube, covering prompting, studying, writing and work uses, and some learning platforms offer Hindi-language AI courses. ChatGPT itself also understands and replies in Hindi.

Also asked as: chatgpt course free hindi

Choose recent tutorials, since features change quickly, and prefer instructors who show real tasks rather than hype. A practical approach: learn prompting basics in Hindi, then practise with your own tasks, asking ChatGPT to explain concepts in Hindi or Hinglish. For deeper learning, combine Hindi tutorials with official English documentation, which is usually most current.
