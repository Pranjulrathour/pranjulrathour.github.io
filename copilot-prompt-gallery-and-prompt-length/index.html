Where is the Copilot Prompt Gallery? Prompt length, system prompts and prompt engineers

Where to find the Copilot Prompt Gallery, why prompting matters for Copilot, how long prompts and system prompts should be, and what prompt engineers do.

Key takeaways

  • Microsoft's Copilot Prompt Gallery collects ready-made prompts for Microsoft 365 Copilot, available in the Copilot app and on the web.
  • Prompts matter for Copilot because it needs the goal, context and source to find the right work content.
  • A prompt should be as long as needed to be clear; system prompts stay effective when every line earns its place.
  • Anthropic publishes the system prompts used in its Claude apps; they are long and detailed.
  • Prompt engineering basics take days; mastery comes from months of building and evaluating real applications.

The Copilot Prompt Gallery is Microsoft's collection of ready-made prompts for Microsoft 365 Copilot, organised by app, task and role, which you can open from the Copilot app or on the web, try and save. This page explains where to find it and why prompting matters so much for Copilot, then answers practical questions about length: how long a prompt, a system prompt, Claude's own system prompt and a creative writing prompt should be. It ends with how long it takes to learn prompt engineering and what prompt engineers do, including in India.

Where is the Copilot Prompt Gallery?

The Copilot Prompt Gallery is available in the Microsoft 365 Copilot app and on the web, and you can also open it from Copilot inside apps such as Word, Teams and Outlook, often through a "view prompts" option. It was previously called Copilot Lab.

Also asked as: where copilot prompt gallery

In the gallery you can filter prompts by app, such as Excel or Teams, by task, such as summarise or create, and by role. You can save your favourite prompts and, in many organisations, share prompts with colleagues. Using it is a fast way to learn what Copilot can do: try a prompt as written, then adapt it with your own files and goals. Availability depends on your licence and organisation settings.

Why is prompt engineering important for Copilot?

Copilot answers from your organisation's content, so a good prompt tells it what you want, which content to use and what form the answer should take. Vague prompts make Copilot search broadly and guess.

Microsoft's guidance on Copilot prompts centres on four elements: the goal, the context, the source to use, and your expectations for the answer. For example, "Summarise the decisions from yesterday's project sync meeting and the follow-up email from the client, as five bullet points with owners" works far better than "what happened yesterday?". Referencing specific files, people or meetings directly improves grounding. Good prompts also reduce errors, because Copilot has less room to fill gaps with assumptions.

How long should a prompt be?

As long as needed to be clear, and no longer. A simple question can be one line; a complex task may need a few paragraphs with context, constraints, examples and the output format. Clarity matters more than length.

Rules of thumb:

  • Include the goal, audience, constraints and format.
  • Add the material to work on, such as a document or data, which can be long.
  • Use examples when format matters.
  • Remove repeated or contradictory instructions.
  • Put the most important instruction clearly, not buried in the middle.

Models accept very long inputs, but a long prompt full of loosely related text can dilute what matters. If a prompt keeps growing, split the task into steps.

How long is too long for a system prompt?

There is no fixed limit, since modern models accept long system prompts, but a system prompt is too long when instructions start contradicting each other, important rules get lost among details, or performance on your test cases stops improving as you add more.

Signs to trim: the model ignores some rules, behaviour changes unpredictably when you add instructions, or most lines cover rare edge cases. Better structure helps: clear sections for role, rules, tools and output format; examples only where they change behaviour; and moving reference material into retrieval or attached documents rather than the system prompt. Measure with an evaluation set when you add or remove instructions, rather than guessing.

How long is Claude's system prompt?

Anthropic publishes the system prompts used in its Claude apps in its documentation's release notes. They are long, detailed documents covering behaviour, formatting, tool use and safety, and they change as new models and features are released.

These published prompts apply to Claude's own apps, such as claude.ai and the mobile apps, not to the Claude API, where developers write their own system prompts. Reading them is a useful lesson in how a production assistant is specified: clear sections, explicit guidance on edge cases, and descriptions of available tools. They also explain why the same model can behave somewhat differently in the app and through the API.

How long is a writing prompt?

A creative writing prompt is usually short, a sentence or two, sometimes a single line or image, designed to spark ideas rather than specify the piece. Prompts for school or exams may add requirements such as word count, form or audience.

When you give a writing task to an AI model, a longer brief usually works better than a short spark: describe the genre, audience, tone, length, point of view and anything to avoid, and include an example of the style you like. For your own practice, short prompts are best, because they leave room for your ideas. If you are learning to write, use AI for feedback on your drafts rather than for producing the piece.

How long is a prompt engineering course?

Short courses take one to a few hours, such as free courses on prompting for developers. Broader courses that include building applications with LLMs, retrieval and evaluation take several weeks part-time.

Also asked as: how long is prompt engineering course

Course length matters less than practice. A practical path: a few hours on an official prompting guide and a short course, then two to four weeks building one small application, such as a document Q&A bot or an extraction tool, with a test set to measure improvements. Look for recently updated courses, because techniques change as models improve, and prefer ones that include structured outputs and evaluation, not just prompt templates.

How long does it take to master prompt engineering?

The basics take days. Being genuinely skilled, able to design prompts for real applications, measure them, and handle edge cases, takes months of building and evaluating, and it keeps evolving as models change.

What mastery looks like in practice: you can write clear specifications, choose between prompting, retrieval and fine-tuning, design structured outputs, write tool descriptions for agents, build evaluation sets, and diagnose failures from logs. Those skills come from shipping projects. Keep a log of prompts, results and changes for each project; reviewing what worked and why builds judgement faster than reading prompt lists.

What are prompt engineers?

Prompt engineers design, test and refine the instructions, examples and context that make AI models produce reliable results for specific tasks, often inside products. Today the work is usually part of broader roles such as AI engineer, GenAI developer or conversation designer.

Day to day, they write system prompts and tool descriptions, build test sets, run evaluations, analyse failures, and collaborate with domain experts and engineers. In companies building AI products, the skill matters, but standalone "prompt engineer" titles are less common than at first, because the work blends into AI engineering. Students should pair prompting skills with programming, retrieval and evaluation to be employable.

How many prompt engineers are there in India?

There is no reliable public count, because prompt engineering is rarely a separate job title and is usually part of roles such as AI engineer, data scientist or GenAI developer. Job platforms show many listings mentioning prompt engineering as a skill.

What is clear is that demand for people who can build with generative AI is growing in India across startups, IT services firms and product companies. For students, the useful question is not how many prompt engineers exist, but which skills employers ask for: Python, LLM APIs, retrieval-augmented generation, evaluation, deployment and communication. Build a project that demonstrates those, and you will match far more roles than a "prompt engineer" title alone.
