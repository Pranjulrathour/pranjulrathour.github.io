Where do Copilot files go? Uploads, downloads, chat history and limits

Where Copilot stores the files you upload and the ones it creates, how long it keeps chats, why it has limits, and how Copilot Chat compares with ChatGPT.

Key takeaways

  • With a work account, files you upload to Copilot Chat and files it creates for you usually land in your OneDrive; follow the link in the reply to find them.
  • Consumer Copilot keeps files with the conversation; download anything you need, because the chat is not a file store.
  • If Copilot will not give a file, ask for the content in the chat and paste it into Word or Excel, or use Copilot inside those apps.

  • Limits exist because long chats cost more, drift off topic and exceed the model's context window; start a fresh chat per task.

Where Copilot files go depends on the account. With a work or school account, files you upload to Microsoft 365 Copilot Chat and documents it creates are usually stored in your OneDrive. With a personal account, files stay attached to the conversation and generated files are offered as a download link. This page explains Copilot versus Copilot Chat, where uploads, downloads and exports end up, what to do when Copilot will not produce a file, whether it reads Excel, how long it keeps history, why it has limits, and how it compares with ChatGPT.

Copilot vs Copilot Chat: what is the difference?

Microsoft 365 Copilot Chat is the chat assistant included for work and school accounts, grounded in the web and the files you give it. Microsoft 365 Copilot is the paid add-on that also reads your organisation's emails, files and meetings and works inside the Office apps.

Also asked as: how does copilot compare to chat gpt

The practical difference is reach. Copilot Chat answers from public web content and from documents you upload or open, under enterprise data protection. The full Microsoft 365 Copilot can answer "what did my manager say about the launch?" by searching your mailbox, Teams chats and SharePoint, within your permissions, and it adds features such as meeting recaps in Teams and analysis in Excel. Consumer Copilot, for personal Microsoft accounts, is a third product with its own app and limits. Check the Copilot icon and the account shown in the corner to know which one you are using.

Where do Copilot files go?

On work and school accounts, files that Copilot Chat receives or creates are generally saved to your OneDrive, in a folder created for Copilot. On personal accounts, they stay with the conversation and you download what you need.

The quickest way to find a file is the link in Copilot's reply: open it, then use "open file location" or check the file's path in OneDrive. Documents created inside Word, Excel or PowerPoint with Copilot are ordinary files and save wherever you save them, usually OneDrive or SharePoint. Copilot Pages, the shareable canvas for Copilot responses, are stored in Microsoft's cloud and appear in your Microsoft 365 app. Exact folder names and behaviour have changed over time, so search OneDrive for the file name if the link does not show it.

Where does Copilot save files?

Copilot saves files where the surrounding app saves them. In Word, Excel and PowerPoint it is your chosen location, usually OneDrive or SharePoint. In Copilot Chat on a work account it is your OneDrive. In consumer Copilot it is your browser's download folder once you download.

This matters for two reasons. First, Copilot in Excel and Word works best on files saved to OneDrive or SharePoint, because it relies on the cloud copy; local files can limit features. Second, saved files follow normal sharing rules: a file in your OneDrive is private until you share it, and a file in a team SharePoint site is visible to that team. Rename generated files straight away, because default names such as "Document 3" are hard to find later.

Where do files uploaded to Copilot go?

On a work or school account, a file you upload to Copilot Chat is generally stored in your OneDrive so Copilot can read it, and it stays covered by your organisation's security and compliance policies. On a personal account, it is kept with that conversation.

Uploading a file does not make it public or share it with colleagues. For work accounts, Microsoft states that prompts, responses and uploaded content are not used to train the foundation models and remain inside the Microsoft 365 service boundary. Even so, follow your college or company's data policy: do not upload documents you are not allowed to process with AI tools, such as other people's personal data. If you upload sensitive drafts often, clean up the OneDrive folder now and then.

Where do Copilot files export to?

Exports go to the app or folder you choose. Copilot responses can be copied, exported to Word or turned into a Copilot Page; downloaded files go to your browser's downloads folder; and Office documents save to OneDrive or SharePoint by default.

If you want a response as a document, ask Copilot to create a Word document or use the export option under the response, then open it and save it under a proper name. For tables, ask for an Excel file or copy the table into Excel directly; formatting usually survives the paste. On phones, downloads go to the system's download location or the Files app. If an export link expires, which can happen with generated files, ask Copilot to regenerate the file in the same conversation.

Why is Copilot not giving downloadable files?

Usually because the product or plan you are using cannot create that file type in chat, the request did not trigger file creation, or the download link expired. Asking explicitly for a named file type often fixes it.

Also asked as: why copilot is not giving downloadable files

Try these in order:

  • Ask clearly: "Create a Word document named report.docx with this content."
  • Ask for the content as text or a table in the chat, then paste it into Word or Excel yourself.
  • Use Copilot inside the target app instead: Copilot in Word for documents, in Excel for spreadsheets, in PowerPoint for decks.
  • Start a new conversation if the old one is long; long chats behave less reliably.
  • Check whether your organisation has disabled file generation.

Copying the content is always the fallback, and it is often faster than fighting the download.

Can Copilot read Excel files?

Yes. You can upload an Excel file to Copilot Chat and ask questions about it, and Copilot inside Excel analyses the open workbook directly: summaries, formulas, charts, PivotTables and trends.

For the best results inside Excel, format the data as a table with Ctrl+T, give each column a clear header, remove merged cells and blank rows, and save the file to OneDrive or SharePoint. Then ask specific questions, such as "which product had the largest month-on-month drop?". In chat, very large workbooks may be summarised rather than read in full, so share a focused sheet. Check any formula Copilot writes on a few rows by hand before trusting the totals.

How long does Copilot keep chat history?

Consumer Copilot keeps your conversations until you delete them, either one by one or all at once in privacy settings. Work and school accounts keep Copilot interactions according to your organisation's retention policies, which admins set.

On work accounts, Copilot interactions are stored within the organisation's Microsoft 365 environment so they can be covered by compliance features such as retention and eDiscovery, the same way email is. Deleting a conversation from your view may not remove the organisation's record if a retention policy applies. On personal accounts you control the history yourself. Either way, assume anything you type may be kept and read later, and keep passwords, keys and personal identity numbers out of every chat.

Why does Copilot have a chat limit?

Limits keep the service affordable and reliable. Every reply costs computing time, very long conversations exceed what the model can hold in context, and long chats tend to drift and give worse answers.

A model can only consider a fixed amount of text at once, its context window. In a long chat, older messages are dropped or summarised, so Copilot can forget instructions you gave at the start. Limits on turns per conversation or requests per day also protect the service at busy times and differ by account type. Work with the limits rather than against them: one conversation per task, a short summary of the important facts when you start a new chat, and the key instructions repeated in your first message.

How does Copilot compare to ChatGPT?

Both are capable assistants, and Copilot uses OpenAI's GPT models for much of its work. The difference is integration: Copilot is built into Windows, Edge and Microsoft 365, while ChatGPT is a standalone app with its own tools.

Choose Copilot if your work happens in Outlook, Word, Excel and Teams, or your organisation provides it with enterprise data protection, because it can see the documents you are working on. Choose ChatGPT if you want one broad assistant with strong voice mode, image generation, custom GPTs and many third-party apps. Many people use both: Copilot for work documents, ChatGPT for learning and side projects. Test both on the same real task before deciding, because model updates change the balance every few months.
