What is a website developer? Hiring one, and building a developer portfolio website

What a website developer does, whether small businesses need one, how to find and hire one, and how to build a strong developer portfolio site with examples.

Key takeaways

  • A website developer builds and maintains websites: front end, back end, or both.
  • Small businesses can start with a website builder; hire a developer for custom features, integrations or performance.
  • Hire by reviewing live work, asking about process and maintenance, and starting with a small paid milestone.
  • A strong developer portfolio shows three to five real projects with problems, decisions, results and links.
  • Free templates and hosting such as GitHub Pages or Vercel are enough; content matters more than design flourishes.

A website developer is someone who builds and maintains websites, writing the code for what visitors see, the front end, and often the servers, databases and integrations behind it, the back end. Some also design; others work from a designer's mockups. I build web products with Next.js, FastAPI and PostgreSQL and run my own site at pranjulrathour.scult.in, so this page covers what developers do, when a business needs one, how to find and hire one, and how to build a developer portfolio website.

What is a website developer?

A website developer writes and maintains the code that makes websites work. Front-end developers build the interface with HTML, CSS and JavaScript frameworks; back-end developers build servers, APIs and databases; full-stack developers do both.

Also asked as: what is website developer

Typical work includes turning designs into responsive pages, connecting forms and payments, building content management, optimising speed and search visibility, ensuring accessibility, deploying to hosting, and fixing issues over time. A web designer focuses on layout, visuals and user experience, though many professionals combine both roles. Developers also make decisions about security, performance and maintainability that visitors never see but that determine whether a site keeps working.

Does a small business need a website developer?

Not always. Many small businesses can start with a website builder such as Wix, Squarespace, Shopify or WordPress.com and do it themselves. A developer becomes worthwhile when you need custom features, integrations, better performance, or a site that must scale.

Also asked as: does small business need a website developer

Signs you need one: booking or ordering flows that builders cannot handle, integration with your CRM or inventory system, slow pages hurting conversions, accessibility requirements, multilingual content, or custom web applications. A good middle path is having a developer set up a solid template, analytics and search basics, then editing content yourself. Whatever you choose, make sure you own the domain and have admin access to everything.

How do I find a website developer?

Look for developers through referrals from businesses whose sites you like, freelance platforms such as Upwork and Toptal, local tech communities and college clubs, LinkedIn and GitHub, and agencies for larger projects.

Also asked as: how to find website developer

Shortlist three to five people whose past work matches what you need, such as e-commerce or booking sites. Check that their sites are fast on mobile, look professional and still work. Ask previous clients about communication, deadlines and support after launch. Student developers can be a good fit for small, well-scoped projects, with clear expectations; I have seen strong work come out of hackathon teams and student communities when the brief is clear.

How do I hire a website designer and developer?

Write a clear brief, compare proposals from a few candidates, review live work and references, agree on scope, milestones and ownership in writing, and start with a small paid milestone before committing to the whole project.

Also asked as: how to hire website designer and developer

Your brief should include goals, pages, features, examples you like, content responsibilities, deadline and budget range. In interviews, ask how they handle design approval, testing, hosting, security updates and handover. Insist that you own the domain, hosting account, code repository and design files. Agree on support after launch, since websites need updates. Avoid choosing purely on lowest cost; poorly built sites often cost more to fix later.

How do I find and evaluate a website developer's portfolio?

Most developers link portfolios from their LinkedIn, GitHub or freelance profiles, or share them when asked. Evaluate by opening the live sites, checking them on a phone, and asking what exactly the developer built on each.

Also asked as: how to find website developer portfolio

What to check:

  • Live, working links rather than only screenshots.
  • Speed and layout on mobile.
  • Variety relevant to your project, such as e-commerce or booking.
  • Case studies explaining problems, decisions and results.
  • Code samples on GitHub for technical roles.

Ask the developer to walk you through one project; clear explanations of trade-offs show real ownership of the work.

How do I build a developer portfolio website?

Pick three to five of your best projects, write a short case study for each, build a simple, fast site with a clear introduction, projects, about and contact sections, and deploy it free on GitHub Pages, Vercel or Netlify with your own domain if possible.

Also asked as: example developer portfolio website

Steps:

  • Write content first: who you are, what you build, your best work.
  • For each project: the problem, your role, stack, key decisions, results, live and code links.
  • Use a framework you know, such as plain HTML, React or Next.js.
  • Make it responsive, accessible and fast.
  • Add a clear way to contact or hire you.

My own site has project pages plus separate invite and hire pages, so visitors with different goals each find a clear next step.

What makes the best developer portfolio website?

The best developer portfolios are clear, fast and specific: within seconds a visitor knows who you are, what you build, and can see working projects with evidence of impact. Design polish helps, but content and proof matter most.

Strong portfolios share traits: a one-line positioning statement, three to five deep projects instead of twenty shallow ones, case studies with real numbers such as users, speed gains or awards, visible contact details, and up-to-date work. Weak portfolios have broken links, tutorial clones presented as original projects, slow animations and no explanation of the developer's role. Recruiters often spend under a minute, so make the first screen count.

What are good examples of developer portfolio websites?

Good examples are easy to find through curated lists such as the "awesome developer portfolios" collections on GitHub, discussions of well-designed personal sites on Hacker News, and the personal sites of engineers you admire.

When studying examples, look past the visuals and note structure: how they introduce themselves, how many projects they show, how they write case studies, and how they invite contact. Some engineers use minimal text-first sites with writing; others build interactive experiences that demonstrate front-end skill. Choose a style that matches the work you want. Never copy someone's site directly; adapt ideas and make the content yours.

What are good ideas for a software developer portfolio website?

Good ideas make the portfolio itself show your skills: a blog with technical write-ups, interactive demos of your projects, a "now" page about current work, a uses page listing your tools, and case studies with architecture diagrams.

Also asked as: software developer portfolio website ideas

More ideas:

  • An AI chat that answers questions about your work, grounded in your own content.
  • Live project status or GitHub activity.
  • A talks and community section with events and slides.
  • A timeline of your journey with milestones.
  • A resume page that is also downloadable.

Pick ideas that support your goal, such as getting hired, finding clients or speaking invitations, rather than adding features for their own sake.

Where can I find a free developer portfolio website template?

Free templates are available from Vercel's template gallery, Netlify and GitHub Pages themes, open-source repositories on GitHub tagged "portfolio template", HTML5 UP, and starter themes for frameworks such as Astro, Next.js and Hugo.

Also asked as: developer portfolio website template free

Choose a template that is lightweight, responsive and easy to edit, check its licence, and customise colours, fonts and layout so it does not look identical to thousands of others. Deploy free with GitHub Pages, Vercel or Netlify. Templates save time, but the content, your projects, explanations and results, is what earns interviews. Update it whenever you finish something meaningful.
