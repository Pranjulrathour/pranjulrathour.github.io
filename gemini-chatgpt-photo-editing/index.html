How to edit photos with Gemini or ChatGPT: prompts, best apps, saving, and fixes

Key takeaways

  • Upload a photo to Gemini or ChatGPT, describe one change at a time, and say what must stay the same.
  • Good prompts specify the subject, change, style, lighting and what to preserve, such as face and pose.
  • Gemini images appear in the chat and can be downloaded to your device; they are also kept in your chat history.
  • Failures usually come from content policies, unclear prompts, limits or uploads; rephrase or try again later.
  • You can write prompts in Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam or Hindi; English often gives more precise edits.

To edit a photo with Gemini or ChatGPT, upload the image, describe the change in plain words, such as "change the background to a library and keep my face exactly the same", then refine with follow-up instructions until it looks right, and download the result. Both handle background changes, style transfers, object edits and enhancements. I build AI products across Google and OpenAI models, so this page covers editing with Gemini and ChatGPT, prompts, the best apps, where photos save, fixing failures, and prompting in Indian languages.

How do I edit photos with Gemini?

Open the Gemini app or website, upload or attach your photo, and type the edit you want. Gemini returns an edited image; continue the conversation to adjust details, and download the version you like.

Tips for better edits:

  • Make one change per prompt: background first, then lighting, then style.
  • State what to keep: "keep the face, hairstyle and clothing unchanged".
  • Describe the setting and mood specifically.
  • Upload a reference image when you want a particular style or object.
  • Start a new chat if results drift after many edits.

Edited images include an invisible SynthID watermark and sometimes a visible mark identifying AI editing.

What are the best Gemini prompts for photo editing?

The best prompts describe the subject, the exact change, the style, lighting and composition, and what must stay identical. Specific, concrete wording works far better than "make it better".

Examples:

  • "Replace the background with a softly blurred college auditorium; keep my face, pose and clothes unchanged."
  • "Turn this photo into a professional LinkedIn headshot: neutral grey background, soft studio lighting, same face."
  • "Restore this old photo: fix scratches and fading, keep it black and white."
  • "Make this product photo look like a clean studio shot on a white background."
  • "Convert this picture into a watercolour illustration with warm colours."

Save prompts that work and reuse them.

How do I create a photo with Gemini?

Type a description of the image you want in the Gemini app, such as "a student presenting a robotics project at a college fest, natural daylight, candid photo style", and Gemini generates it. Refine with follow-ups, such as changing angle, colours or mood.

Also asked as: how to gemini photo · where gemini photo save

For realistic results, mention the camera style, lighting, setting, time of day and level of detail. For illustrations, name the art style. Avoid requesting images of real, identifiable people without consent, and do not use generated images to mislead. Image generation counts against your plan's limits, so detailed first prompts save attempts.

How do I edit photos with ChatGPT?

In ChatGPT, upload your photo, describe the edit, and ChatGPT generates an edited version using its image model. Ask follow-up questions to refine, and download the final image from the chat.

ChatGPT is good at style transformations, adding or removing elements, background replacement, and creating variations. For precise edits, select or describe exact regions, such as "only change the sky". Results may alter details you did not mention, so state what to preserve. Generated images follow OpenAI's content policies and may carry provenance metadata indicating AI generation.

What are good ChatGPT photo editing prompts?

Effective ChatGPT prompts mirror Gemini's: be specific about the change, the style and what to preserve, and iterate one change at a time.

Also asked as: how to chatgpt photo editing · how to chatgpt photo editing prompts

Examples:

  • "Change the lighting to golden hour sunset; keep everything else identical."
  • "Remove the people in the background and fill naturally."
  • "Make this look like a cinematic film still with teal and orange colour grading."
  • "Create a cartoon avatar of this photo in a flat illustration style."
  • "Expand this image to a wider 16:9 frame, continuing the scenery naturally."

If an edit goes wrong, describe precisely what to fix rather than starting over.

Which AI app is best for photo editing?

It depends on the task. Gemini and ChatGPT are excellent for conversational edits and creative changes; Adobe Photoshop and Firefly offer precise generative fill with professional control; Google Photos' Magic Editor and Snapseed suit quick phone edits; and Canva works well for social media designs.

For professional work needing accuracy, use Photoshop or Lightroom with AI features. For quick, creative transformations, Gemini or ChatGPT are fastest. For privacy-sensitive images, prefer on-device editing tools. Check each tool's terms on commercial use of generated or edited images, especially for client work.

Where does Gemini save photos?

Gemini shows generated and edited images in the chat, where they stay in your conversation history while that chat exists. To keep a copy on your device, use the download button on the image; on phones, it saves to your gallery or downloads folder.

If you cannot find an image, open the conversation from Gemini's chat history. Images are not automatically added to Google Photos unless you save them there. If your Gemini activity settings delete chats after a period, download important images promptly. On shared devices, remember that chat history may be visible to others using your account.

Why is Gemini not creating photos?

Common reasons: the request conflicts with content policies, such as images of real people or sensitive topics; you reached your daily image limit; image generation is not available in your region, language or account type; or there is a temporary service problem.

Also asked as: why gemini is not creating photo

Try rephrasing the prompt to remove restricted elements, check whether a limit message appears, update the app, switch between the app and web, and try again later. Some school and work accounts have image generation disabled by administrators. If Gemini replies with text instead of an image, ask explicitly: "Generate an image of...".

Why is Gemini not editing my photos?

Gemini may refuse or fail to edit photos when the edit involves identifiable people in restricted ways, when the upload is too large or in an unsupported format, when you hit usage limits, or when the instruction is ambiguous. Sometimes it generates a new image instead of editing.

Also asked as: why gemini is not editing my photos

Fixes: upload a smaller JPEG or PNG, give one clear instruction, mention "edit this photo" explicitly, and start a new chat if the conversation is long. Check whether your account or region supports image editing. Avoid edits that could create misleading or harmful images of real people, which policies block.

How do I edit photos with Gemini in Tamil or other Indian languages?

You can write your prompt in Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam, Hindi and many other languages Gemini supports, describing the edit just as you would in English. Gemini understands the request and performs the edit.

For very precise edits, some users find English prompts give more consistent results, because image models are often trained with more English descriptions. A practical approach: describe the edit in your language, and if the result is not right, try the same prompt in English or mix key terms, such as "background blur". Many Indian creators share prompt ideas in their languages, which help with local styles such as traditional attire and festival themes.
