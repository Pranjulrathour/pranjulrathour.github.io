Can Gemini transcribe audio? Files, M4A, video, and ChatGPT or Copilot

Gemini can transcribe audio files and the audio in videos. Supported formats, M4A tips, how ChatGPT and Copilot compare, and Gemini vs GPT for everyday use.

Key takeaways

  • Gemini accepts audio input, so it can transcribe, summarise and translate recordings in the app or through the API.
  • If an M4A file will not upload, convert it to MP3 or WAV with one ffmpeg command.
  • For long or sensitive recordings, a dedicated speech-to-text tool or local Whisper gives more control.
  • ChatGPT transcribes voice input and uploaded audio within limits; Microsoft's Word and Teams transcribe files and meetings.
  • Gemini and GPT are close in quality; choose by ecosystem and test on your own audio.

Yes, Gemini can transcribe audio. Its models accept audio as input, so you can upload a recording in the Gemini app or send it through the Gemini API and ask for a transcript, with timestamps, speaker labels, a summary or a translation. It can also transcribe the audio track of a video. This page covers files and formats including M4A, video, how ChatGPT and Copilot handle transcription, how Gemini compares with GPT, which Gemini model to use for images, where Gemini is available, and practical examples.

Can Gemini transcribe audio?

Yes. Upload a recording or record directly, then ask Gemini to transcribe it. Because Gemini understands the content, you can ask for more than a transcript: clean it up, label speakers, summarise, list action items or translate.

A good prompt: "Transcribe this recording verbatim with timestamps every minute and label speakers as Speaker 1 and Speaker 2. Then give a five-bullet summary." Check names, numbers and technical terms, the most common errors in any transcription. For very long recordings, the API handles large files better than the app, and dedicated speech-to-text services offer features such as guaranteed verbatim output and streaming. I built live speech-to-text into my OCR & Speech Workspace for this reason: transcripts turn speech into something you can search and summarise.

Can Gemini transcribe audio files?

Yes. In the Gemini app, attach the audio file and ask for a transcript. Through the Gemini API, upload the file with the Files API and pass it with your prompt. Common formats such as MP3, WAV, AAC, FLAC and OGG are supported.

Using the API from Python:

from google import genai
client = genai.Client()
audio = client.files.upload(file="lecture.mp3")
resp = client.models.generate_content(
    model="gemini-2.5-flash",
    contents=["Transcribe this lecture with timestamps.", audio])
print(resp.text)

There are limits on file size and total audio length per request; the audio documentation lists them. Split very long recordings into parts and combine the transcripts.

Can Gemini transcribe M4A audio?

M4A files contain AAC audio, which Gemini generally handles, but uploads of the M4A container itself sometimes fail depending on where you upload. If that happens, convert the file to MP3 or WAV, which work reliably.

Also asked as: can gemini transcribe audio m4a

Convert with one ffmpeg command:

ffmpeg -i recording.m4a -ac 1 -ar 16000 recording.mp3

That also reduces the file to mono at 16 kHz, which is plenty for speech and makes uploads faster. Phone voice memos are often M4A, so this is a common fix. If you do not use the command line, many free audio converters do the same thing; avoid uploading private recordings to unknown converter websites.

Can Gemini transcribe audio from a video?

Yes. Gemini accepts video files and YouTube links in many cases, and can transcribe the speech, describe what is shown, and give timestamps. You can also extract the audio and send only that, which is faster and uses less of your limits.

Also asked as: can gemini transcribe audio from video

To extract audio:

ffmpeg -i talk.mp4 -vn -ac 1 -ar 16000 talk.mp3

Then ask for a transcript or chapter list. When you need both speech and visuals, such as a lecture where slides matter, send the video and ask Gemini to note slide titles alongside the transcript. For YouTube videos with captions, those captions may already be a good starting transcript.

Can ChatGPT transcribe audio?

Yes, in several ways. ChatGPT transcribes your voice input when you dictate, voice mode understands speech in conversation, and uploaded audio can be transcribed depending on the tools and limits of your plan. OpenAI's API offers dedicated transcription models.

For reliable transcripts of files, developers use OpenAI's speech-to-text API or the open-source Whisper model, which runs locally for free. In the ChatGPT app, short recordings work best; long meetings are better handled by a transcription tool, with ChatGPT then summarising the transcript. As with Gemini, check names and numbers. Do not upload recordings of other people without their consent.

Will Copilot transcribe audio?

Microsoft's tools transcribe audio, though not always through the Copilot chat itself. Teams transcribes meetings live, Word's Transcribe feature turns uploaded recordings into text with speakers and timestamps, and Copilot can then summarise those transcripts.

In Word for the web, use Dictate, then Transcribe, upload an audio file, and insert the transcript into your document. In Teams meetings, turn on transcription, and Copilot can answer questions about the meeting and create a recap. For other cases, generate a transcript with one of these tools or with Whisper, then paste it into Copilot for summaries and action items. Availability depends on your Microsoft 365 plan and organisation settings.

Gemini vs GPT: which is better?

Gemini and OpenAI's GPT models are close in overall quality, and leadership changes with each release. Gemini is strong on long, multimodal inputs such as audio and video and is built into Google's apps; GPT models power ChatGPT's broad ecosystem.

For transcription and media work, Gemini's native audio and video input is an advantage. For writing, coding and general assistance, both are excellent, and the better choice depends on your tasks and tools. Developers should compare cost, speed, rate limits and features such as structured outputs for their own workload. Run the same five real tasks on both and keep the one whose answers need less fixing.

Which Gemini version is best for image generation?

Use Google's Gemini image models, the family nicknamed Nano Banana, for creating and editing images in conversation, and Google's Imagen models for high-quality text-to-image generation through the API. The newest versions are usually the best.

Gemini's image models are good at editing existing photos with instructions, keeping a character consistent across images, and combining several images. Imagen focuses on generating high-quality images from text. In the Gemini app, image creation picks a model for you; in the API and AI Studio you choose. Check the image generation documentation for current model names, and label AI-generated images when they could be mistaken for real photos.

Where is Gemini available?

Gemini is available in most countries and in many languages, on the web, Android and iOS, and inside Google Workspace. Availability of some features, such as image generation, Gemini Live or specific integrations, varies by country, age and account type.

In India, Gemini supports English and several Indian languages and is widely available on Android, where it is replacing Google Assistant on many phones. Some regions have limited or no access because of local regulation or Google's rollout decisions. Google's Gemini help centre lists supported countries and languages. Work and school accounts depend on the administrator enabling Gemini. If a feature is missing, check your account type, age settings and app version before assuming it is unavailable.

What are some examples of using Gemini AI?

People use Gemini to summarise long documents and videos, transcribe and summarise recordings, plan study schedules and trips, draft emails and reports, explain code, generate and edit images, and research topics with cited sources.

Also asked as: examples gemini ai

Examples to try:

  • "Transcribe this voice note and turn it into a to-do list with deadlines."
  • "Summarise this YouTube lecture into revision notes with timestamps."
  • "Here is my project report; point out unclear sections and missing evidence."
  • "Plan a one-week revision schedule for these five subjects."
  • "Explain this error and the fix, step by step."
  • "Edit this photo to a plain white background for my resume."

Check facts and names in every output before relying on it.
