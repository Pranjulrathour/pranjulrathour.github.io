Key takeaways

  • ChatGPT launched on 30 November 2022 and reached mass adoption within weeks.
  • The official site is chatgpt.com; many look-alike sites and apps are unofficial and risky.
  • ChatGPT can create PowerPoint files, and its agent mode can browse, use tools and complete multi-step tasks.
  • Answers come from patterns learned in training plus web search and files you provide, so verify important facts.
  • Astra is the name of Google DeepMind's assistant project; treat unverified 'ChatGPT 6 Astra' claims with caution.

ChatGPT, OpenAI's AI assistant, launched on 30 November 2022 and became one of the fastest-growing consumer apps ever. Today it writes, analyses files, searches the web, creates images and documents, and runs agent tasks. I build AI products across several model providers, so this page answers common ChatGPT questions: its launch, response length, official sites, making PowerPoint files, how agents work and building your own, water use, where its information comes from, Apple Intelligence, and claims about "ChatGPT 6 Astra".

When was ChatGPT launched?

OpenAI launched ChatGPT as a free research preview on 30 November 2022. It reached an estimated 100 million users within about two months, making it one of the fastest-adopted consumer applications in history.

Also asked as: when chat gpt was launched

It was first based on a model from the GPT-3.5 series, fine-tuned for conversation with reinforcement learning from human feedback. Paid plans, GPT-4 access, plugins, image generation, voice, custom GPTs, search and agent features followed over the next years. ChatGPT's launch triggered the wave of generative AI products and investment that shaped the technology industry since. For students and developers, it also changed how people learn to code, research topics and draft documents, which is why AI literacy is now treated as a basic skill.

How long can ChatGPT answers and chats be?

ChatGPT's response length is limited by the model's output token limit and the conversation's context window, both of which vary by model and plan. Long answers may stop partway; asking it to continue usually resumes them.

Also asked as: how long chat gpt

For long documents, ask for an outline first, then generate section by section, or use Canvas for long-form editing. Very long conversations eventually exceed the context window, and earlier details may be forgotten or summarised; start a new chat with a summary when that happens. Developers using the API set maximum output tokens explicitly and pay for input and output tokens.

What are the official ChatGPT sites?

The official ChatGPT site is chatgpt.com, and OpenAI's official apps are available in Apple's App Store, Google Play and as desktop apps for macOS and Windows from OpenAI. OpenAI's company site is openai.com.

Also asked as: what are chat gpt sites

Many websites and apps use "ChatGPT" in their names without being OpenAI products; some are legitimate tools using OpenAI's API, while others are scams that collect data or payments. Check the publisher name in app stores, avoid entering passwords or payment details on look-alike sites, and use official sources for downloads and subscriptions.

Can ChatGPT make a PPT?

Yes. ChatGPT can generate PowerPoint files by writing and running code, producing a downloadable .pptx with slides, titles, bullet points and simple layouts, and its agent mode can create presentations as part of larger tasks.

Also asked as: can chat gpt make ppt

For best results, agree on the outline first, then ask for the deck with specifics: number of slides, audience, style, speaker notes and any data or charts. Generated decks usually need design polish in PowerPoint or Google Slides. Check facts and figures carefully, and add your own visuals and examples so the presentation reflects your voice. If your college or company has a slide template, upload it and ask ChatGPT to follow its layout to save formatting time.

How do ChatGPT agents work?

ChatGPT's agent mode gives the model a virtual computer with a browser, terminal and connectors, so it can plan and carry out multi-step tasks, such as researching options, filling forms or creating documents, while asking permission before consequential actions.

Also asked as: how do chat gpt agents work

It combines web browsing, code execution and integration with connected apps, deciding which tool to use at each step and showing its progress. You can interrupt, take over or redirect at any time. As with any agent, results need review, sites may block automated browsing, and prompt injection on web pages is a risk, so avoid giving it sensitive credentials or unrestricted access.

Can you create AI agents in ChatGPT?

You can create custom GPTs in ChatGPT with instructions, knowledge files and actions that call external APIs, which act as specialised assistants. For developers, OpenAI also provides tools and APIs to build agents with tool use, orchestration and evaluation.

Also asked as: can you create ai agents in chat gpt

A custom GPT suits repeatable assistants, such as a study helper or a company FAQ bot. For agents that run workflows across systems, use OpenAI's developer tools, other agent frameworks such as LangGraph, or automation platforms such as n8n. Start with a narrow task, define tools carefully, add approval steps, and test with real examples before sharing.

Why does ChatGPT use water?

ChatGPT runs in data centres whose servers produce heat, and many data centres use water for cooling, directly through evaporative cooling or indirectly through electricity generation. Each query therefore has a small water and energy footprint.

Also asked as: why does chat gpt use water

In 2025, OpenAI's chief executive estimated that an average ChatGPT query uses a tiny fraction of a teaspoon of water and a fraction of a watt-hour of energy, though methods and assumptions vary across studies. Training large models and generating images or video use much more. Totals matter at global scale, which is why companies report on efficiency, renewable energy and water recycling.

Where does ChatGPT get its information?

ChatGPT's knowledge comes from training on large datasets, including publicly available internet text, licensed data and data created by human trainers, up to a cut-off date. When search is used, it also retrieves current web pages and cites them. Files you upload are another source.

Also asked as: where chat gpt get the information

It does not look facts up in a database by default; it generates answers from learned patterns, which is why it can be confidently wrong. Use search mode and ask for sources when facts matter, and check the cited pages. For your own organisation's information, grounded systems such as RAG, which answer only from your documents with citations, are more reliable.

Can you use Apple AI like ChatGPT?

Partly. Apple Intelligence offers writing tools, summaries, image features and a more capable Siri on supported devices, focused on personal tasks and privacy. For open-ended conversations and broad questions, it can hand requests to ChatGPT through Apple's integration, with your permission.

Also asked as: can you use apple ai like chat gpt

So Apple Intelligence is not a direct ChatGPT replacement, but it covers many everyday tasks inside apps, and the ChatGPT integration fills gaps. You can use the integration without an OpenAI account, or sign in to use your plan's features. Supported devices, languages and regions are limited, so check Apple's availability list.

What is ChatGPT 6 Astra?

Treat "ChatGPT 6 Astra" claims with caution: OpenAI's official announcements are the reliable source for its model names and releases, and the "Astra" name is best known from Google DeepMind's Project Astra, a research prototype of a universal AI assistant that sees and hears through a device's camera and microphone.

Also asked as: what is chat gpt 6 astra

Rumoured model names often spread on social media and video channels before or without any official confirmation, sometimes mixing names from different companies. Check openai.com and OpenAI's release notes for current ChatGPT models, and Google DeepMind's pages for Project Astra. Be wary of sites selling access to unannounced "next" models, which are often scams.
