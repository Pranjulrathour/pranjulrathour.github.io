Fresher resume guide: format, pages, mobile, objective, summary, photo, declaration

Key takeaways

  • A fresher resume should be one page, single-column and focused on projects, skills and results.
  • Reverse-chronological or combination formats work best, with projects near the top for tech roles.
  • Skip generic objectives, and use a short evidence-based summary only if it adds value.
  • Photos and declarations are optional, so include them only when an employer asks.
  • Show strengths and achievements through specific, verifiable results rather than adjectives.

A fresher resume has one job: convince a recruiter in a few seconds that you have the skills and potential for the role, even without full-time experience. Projects, internships and achievements carry the weight that work history carries for experienced candidates. I have mentored more than 200 students, many preparing for placements, so this page covers making a professional fresher resume, the best format, length, building it on a phone, objective, summary, photo, declaration, achievements and strengths.

How do you make a professional resume for freshers?

Use a clean one-page layout with your name and contact links at the top, then education, skills, projects, internships, achievements and certifications. Write concise bullet points that show what you built and the result, tailored to each job.

Also asked as: how to make professional resume for freshers

Start each bullet with a strong verb, such as built, designed or improved, and add specific technologies and outcomes, such as users served or time saved, where honest. Link your GitHub, portfolio and LinkedIn. Use a simple font, consistent spacing and clear headings so applicant tracking systems can read it. Keep school details to class 10 and 12 results unless more is requested. Save as a PDF with a professional file name. Ask a senior or mentor to review it, and update it after every new project or internship.

Which type of resume is best for freshers?

A reverse-chronological or combination resume works best for freshers: education and projects first, listed newest first, with a strong skills section. Avoid purely functional resumes, which hide timelines and make recruiters suspicious.

For technical roles, place projects above education if they are your strongest proof, since recruiters scan for hands-on experience. For non-technical roles, highlight internships, leadership in clubs and relevant coursework. Choose a single-column layout over complex two-column designs with graphics, since applicant tracking systems may misread them. Templates such as Jake's resume on Overleaf are popular with engineering students for being clean and ATS-friendly. Whatever format you choose, keep it consistent, readable and focused on the target role.

How many pages should a resume for freshers be?

One page. Freshers rarely have enough relevant experience to justify more, and recruiters spend only seconds on a first scan, so a tight single page with your strongest projects and skills works best.

If content spills over, cut weaker items rather than shrinking fonts or margins: remove school activities, generic skills such as MS Office, objective statements that say little, and duplicate projects. Keep the three or four most impressive projects with detail rather than listing ten briefly. Some government and academic applications ask for detailed CVs with more pages, so follow their instructions. Research positions may need a list of publications. For most company applications, though, one focused page signals that you can prioritise what matters.

How do you make a resume for freshers on mobile?

Use a mobile app such as Google Docs, Microsoft Word or Canva, start from a simple resume template, fill in your details, check the layout in print preview, and export it as a PDF to share or upload.

Also asked as: how to make resume for freshers in mobile

Phone screens make formatting harder, so pick a single-column template with few design elements. Type carefully and use voice typing for long sections, then proofread on a larger screen if possible. Overleaf works in a mobile browser for LaTeX templates, though editing is easier on a laptop. Save the PDF with a clear name, such as your name and the word resume, and keep a copy in cloud storage for quick access when applying through job apps. Check that links remain clickable in the exported PDF.

What is an objective in a resume for freshers?

A career objective is one or two sentences at the top stating the role you want and what you offer, such as an entry-level backend developer role using Python and FastAPI to build reliable APIs.

Also asked as: what is objective in resume for freshers

Generic objectives, such as seeking a challenging position in a reputed organisation, waste space and are ignored by recruiters. If you include one, make it specific to the role and company, mention your strongest skills, and keep it short. Many recruiters now prefer a brief professional summary instead, which focuses on what you bring rather than what you want. For freshers changing fields, an objective can usefully explain the switch. Otherwise, use the space for projects, which prove skills far better than statements.

Is a summary necessary in a resume for freshers?

Not strictly, but a sharp two or three line summary helps when it highlights your strongest skills and proof, such as projects, internships or hackathon wins. Skip it if it would only repeat generic phrases.

Also asked as: is summary necessary in resume for freshers

A good summary reads like a headline: a final-year computer applications student who built and deployed three full-stack apps with Next.js and FastAPI and won a national hackathon. It gives recruiters a reason to read further. Tailor it to each role by emphasising matching skills. Avoid clichés such as hardworking and passionate without evidence. If space is tight on one page, cut the summary before cutting a strong project. For LinkedIn, a longer summary works well, but keep the resume version brief.

Is a photo necessary in a resume for freshers?

No. Most private-sector and tech employers do not need a photo, and many recommend leaving it out to reduce bias and save space. Include one only if the employer or application explicitly asks.

Also asked as: is photo necessary in resume for freshers

Practices vary by country and sector. In India, photos still appear on many resumes, and some government, hospitality, aviation or modelling roles request them. Applications to companies in the United States, the United Kingdom and many global firms generally discourage photos. If required, use a recent, professional headshot with a plain background, sized small in a corner. Applicant tracking systems ignore photos, and graphics can disrupt parsing. Your LinkedIn profile is the better place for a professional photo.

What is a declaration in a resume for freshers?

A declaration is a closing statement such as I hereby declare that the information above is true to the best of my knowledge, followed by your name, date and place. It is traditional in Indian resumes but optional.

Also asked as: what is declaration in resume for freshers

Most private companies, especially in technology, do not need a declaration, and it uses space that projects could fill. Some government jobs, public sector undertakings and certain traditional employers still expect it, so include it when an application format or recruiter asks. If you add it, keep it to one line with your name. It adds little in practice, since you are expected to provide truthful information regardless. Never include false details in any resume, since background checks often verify education and experience.

What are achievements in a resume for freshers?

Achievements are specific, verifiable accomplishments that set you apart: hackathon wins, competition ranks, scholarships, published projects, open-source contributions, leadership roles and certifications from rigorous programs. Include results, not participation alone.

Also asked as: what are achievements in resume for freshers

Write each with context and scale, such as first prize at a national hackathon among many teams, or a top rank in a coding contest. My own list includes first prizes at Changethon 2025, Product Genesis at Vividhotsava 2025 and BYTEBATTLE, which recruiters can verify. Founding or leading a club, organising events and mentoring students also count, especially with numbers, such as a community grown to more than 500 members. Keep the section short and relevant, and avoid padding it with minor school-level awards.

What are the strengths in a resume for freshers?

Strengths are the skills and qualities you can prove, such as problem solving, quick learning, communication or teamwork. On a resume, show them through projects and achievements rather than listing adjectives in a separate section.

Also asked as: what are the strengths in resume for freshers

Instead of writing strong problem-solving skills, include a bullet showing a problem you solved, such as optimising a slow query or debugging a deployment. Technical strengths belong in a skills section grouped by category, such as languages, frameworks, databases and tools, listing only those you can discuss in an interview. Soft skills shine through leadership roles, team projects and presentations. Prepare to discuss two or three strengths with examples in interviews, since the greatest-strength question is common. Evidence always beats claims.
