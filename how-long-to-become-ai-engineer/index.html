How long does it take to become an AI engineer? Degrees, PCB, self-taught, GenAI, Google

Key takeaways

  • From zero coding, expect 12 to 24 months to become job-ready; working developers can transition in 3 to 6 months.
  • Start with Python and APIs, then build a RAG app, an agent and a deployed product.
  • A CS, AI, BCA plus MCA or B.Sc degree helps, but shipped projects carry great weight in AI engineering.
  • PCB students can become AI engineers through BCA or B.Sc routes, adding mathematics where eligibility requires it.
  • Google hires through coding interviews, ML knowledge and real projects; internships and referrals help.

Becoming an AI engineer usually takes one to two years of focused learning if you start with basic programming, or three to six months if you are already a software developer. AI engineers build products with models: retrieval, agents, fine-tuning, evaluation and deployment. I started BCA in 2023, have led engineering at SCULT INDIA since 2024, and built five AI apps for NextUpgrad in 2026, so this page covers timelines, how to start, self-taught and GenAI paths, degrees, PCB, difficulty, Google and career fit.

How long does it take to become an AI engineer?

From zero programming, expect about 12 to 24 months of consistent study and projects to become job-ready for junior AI engineering roles. A working software developer can transition in roughly 3 to 6 months, because backend, API and deployment skills transfer directly.

Also asked as: how long to become ai engineer

The timeline depends on hours per week, prior maths and coding, and whether you build real projects. A degree takes three or four years but covers broader foundations; self-study can be faster but needs discipline. Milestones help: Python and Git in the first months, then APIs and a first RAG app, then agents, fine-tuning basics and evaluation, then deployment and a portfolio. Being job-ready means you can ship and explain a working AI product, not finish a course list.

How do you start to become an AI engineer?

Start with Python and basic software skills: functions, data structures, Git, APIs and JSON. Then call a model API, build a small chatbot, and expand it into a document question answering app with retrieval and citations.

Also asked as: how to start to become ai engineer

A good first month: learn Python basics, build a command-line app, and make your first API call to a language model. Month two: learn embeddings and vector search and build a RAG app over your notes. Month three: add a web interface with FastAPI or Next.js and deploy it. Along the way, read official documentation, join a community such as a college tech club, and share progress publicly on GitHub so others can review your work.

How do you become a self-taught AI engineer?

Follow a structured roadmap, build projects continuously, and get feedback from real users or developers. Free resources are enough: provider documentation, Hugging Face courses, fast.ai, roadmap.sh's AI engineer roadmap and open-source code.

Also asked as: how to become a self taught ai engineer

Self-taught engineers succeed by proving skills publicly: a portfolio of two or three deployed AI apps with clear READMEs, evaluation results and write-ups of design decisions. Contribute to open-source AI projects, join hackathons to work in teams under deadlines, and find mentors who review your code. The main risk is gaps in fundamentals, so schedule time for data structures, databases, networking and basic statistics. Employers hiring for AI engineering often weigh shipped work as much as degrees.

How do you become a GenAI engineer?

A generative AI engineer builds applications with large language models and other generative models. Learn prompting, RAG, agents and tool calling, fine-tuning with methods like LoRA, evaluation, and deployment, on top of solid Python and backend skills.

Build a portfolio that shows each layer: a RAG app with hybrid retrieval and citations, an agent with tools and guardrails, a fine-tuned small model with a before-and-after evaluation, and a deployed app with monitoring. My NextUpgrad apps follow this pattern: RAG.NextUpgrad for retrieval with reranking and a confidence gate, and FineTune Studio for QLoRA fine-tuning with live loss charts and base-versus-tuned evaluation. Understanding cost, latency and failure modes separates engineers from demo builders.

What degree do you need to become an AI engineer?

Common degrees are B.Tech or B.E. in computer science, AI or data science, BCA followed by MCA, and B.Sc in computer science, mathematics or statistics. A master's helps for research-heavy roles but is not required for most AI engineering jobs.

Also asked as: what degree to become ai engineer

Many applied AI teams care more about whether you can build and ship than about the degree title, though some large companies and government roles filter by degree. Choose a programme that gives you strong programming, data structures, databases, linear algebra, probability and some machine learning. I am a BCA student who leads engineering at SCULT INDIA; the degree gave structure, while projects, hackathons and real products built my AI engineering skills.

Can I become an AI engineer with PCB?

Yes. Students with physics, chemistry and biology in 12th can become AI engineers, though some routes need mathematics. Many BCA and B.Sc programmes accept students from any stream or let them study mathematics later, while most B.Tech programmes require mathematics.

Also asked as: can i become ai engineer with pcb

Options include BCA or B.Sc in computer science or data science at universities that admit PCB students, adding mathematics through a board such as NIOS where eligibility needs it, or a biology-related degree combined with programming, leading to AI in healthcare, bioinformatics or drug discovery. Check each college's eligibility rules before applying. Whatever route you choose, learn Python early and build up mathematics, especially linear algebra and probability, which AI work relies on.

Is it hard to become an AI engineer?

It is challenging but achievable. The difficulty is less about genius and more about consistency: learning programming, some mathematics and many tools, then building projects that work reliably rather than only in demos.

Also asked as: is it hard to become a engineer

The hard parts are debugging, understanding why models fail, evaluating quality, and keeping up with fast-changing tools. The field also rewards communication: explaining trade-offs to teammates and users. What makes it easier is that resources are free and plentiful, communities are welcoming, and you can see progress quickly by building. Studying a little every day and finishing projects beats occasional intense bursts. Mentors and peers shorten the path significantly, so join a community early.

How do you become an AI engineer at Google?

Google hires AI and machine learning engineers through its careers site, usually requiring strong computer science fundamentals, coding interviews on data structures and algorithms, machine learning knowledge, and evidence of building real systems.

Also asked as: how to become ai engineer in google

Prepare on three fronts: practise algorithm problems until you can solve medium-level questions clearly in about 40 minutes, study ML system design and fundamentals, and build substantial projects or open-source contributions. Students can target internships, which often convert into full-time offers. Referrals help your application get seen, so network at conferences, hackathons and developer events such as Google Developer Groups. Many Google AI roles also value research experience or publications, especially in research-focused teams.

Should I become an AI engineer?

Community discussions, such as on Reddit, mostly say yes if you enjoy building software and learning constantly, and no if you only want a trending title. AI engineering is software engineering with models, not a shortcut around it.

Also asked as: how to become gen ai engineer · should i become ai engineer reddit

Common advice from practitioners: learn solid software engineering first, avoid course collecting, build real products, and expect the tools to change every few months. Many warn that entry-level roles are competitive and that strong fundamentals matter more than knowing the latest framework. Choose it if you like debugging, experimenting and explaining your work, and if you are comfortable with uncertainty. If you prefer stable, slowly changing work, data engineering or backend roles may suit you better.

Can anyone become an engineer?

Most people who commit to steady learning can become software or AI engineers; you do not need to be a genius or a topper. What you need is curiosity, persistence, problem-solving practice and enough mathematics for your chosen area.

Backgrounds vary widely: engineers come from commerce, arts and science streams, career changes and self-teaching. Formal engineering titles in some fields, such as civil or electrical, require specific degrees and licences, while software and AI roles are more open to skill-based paths. Start small, build consistently, and seek feedback. I have mentored more than 200 students since 2023, and I tell each of them the same thing: build and ship regularly, because where you start matters less than how consistently you practise.
