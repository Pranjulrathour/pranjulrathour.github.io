Key takeaways

  • roadmap.sh's AI Engineer roadmap is a solid free map: models, APIs, prompting, embeddings, RAG, agents and safety.
  • A practical path is Python and software basics, LLM APIs, RAG, evaluation, agents, then deployment.
  • LLM engineers go deeper into retrieval, fine-tuning, evaluation and serving; agentic engineers into tools and orchestration.
  • AI security engineers study prompt injection, data leakage, model supply chains and the OWASP LLM risks.
  • BCA students can follow the same path, proving skill with deployed projects rather than degree names.

An AI engineer roadmap for 2026 starts with Python and software fundamentals, then moves through language model APIs, prompting, embeddings and RAG, evaluation, agents and tool use, fine-tuning, and deployment with monitoring. roadmap.sh's AI Engineer roadmap maps these topics well. I followed a similar path while studying BCA, shipping five production AI apps in 2026, and gave a three-year roadmap to over 100 first-year students at VSICS, so this page covers roadmap.sh, a complete path, PDFs, GitHub and community advice, specialised tracks, and a BCA plan.

What is the roadmap.sh AI Engineer roadmap?

roadmap.sh is a free, community-driven site with visual learning roadmaps for developer roles, and its AI Engineer roadmap covers what an engineer building AI applications should learn: pretrained models, APIs, prompt engineering, embeddings, vector databases, RAG, agents, multimodal AI, safety and development tools.

Each node links to resources, and you can mark progress with an account. It focuses on using and integrating models rather than researching new architectures, which matches most AI engineering jobs. Treat it as a map, not a checklist to finish before building: pick a project, learn the nodes the project needs, and repeat. roadmap.sh also has separate roadmaps for machine learning, data science and MLOps.

What is a complete AI engineer roadmap?

A complete path has six stages, each ending with a project you can demo. Move on when you can build, not when you finish a course.

Also asked as: data science skills for mca students: a complete learning roadmap · how to become an ai engineer in 2026 the complete learning roadmap

  • Foundations: Python, Git, APIs, SQL, Linux basics, data structures.
  • LLM basics: tokens, prompting, structured output, model APIs and costs.
  • Retrieval: embeddings, chunking, hybrid search, reranking, RAG with citations.
  • Evaluation: test sets, metrics, LLM judges with human checks, tracing.
  • Agents and tools: tool calling, MCP, workflows, guardrails.
  • Production: FastAPI, Docker, cloud, monitoring, security, cost control.

Add fine-tuning with LoRA and QLoRA once you know when prompting and retrieval fall short. My RAG.NextUpgrad and FineTune Studio projects map to stages three and the fine-tuning add-on.

Is there an AI engineer roadmap 2026 PDF?

roadmap.sh lets you download its roadmaps as images or PDFs, and many educators share PDF versions. A PDF is handy for offline planning, but the online version stays current and links to resources.

Also asked as: snapchat machine learning engineer interview guide 2026 · data scientist vs machine learning engineer 2026: the $43k premium

AI tooling changes quickly, so check the year and contents of any PDF: roadmaps from 2023 may omit agents, MCP and modern evaluation, while overemphasising prompt tricks. Use the PDF to plan your months, then turn it into your own document with dates, projects and links to what you built. A personal roadmap with evidence of progress is more useful than any generic chart.

Where can I find an AI engineer roadmap on GitHub?

The roadmap.sh source lives in the developer-roadmap repository on GitHub, one of the most-starred repositories on the platform. Many other repositories collect AI engineering resources, courses and project ideas, often titled "awesome" lists or "AI engineer roadmap".

Also asked as: how to find a good machine learning engineer job

When choosing a GitHub roadmap, prefer ones that are maintained, link to primary sources such as official documentation, and include projects rather than only reading lists. Fork one and adapt it: delete topics irrelevant to your goals, add your own notes and projects, and track progress through commits. Your fork then doubles as visible evidence of consistent learning.

What does the community recommend in AI engineer roadmaps?

Community discussions, such as on Reddit, repeatedly stress a few points: strong software engineering fundamentals matter more than collecting AI certificates, build real projects early, learn evaluation, and avoid spending months on theory before shipping anything.

Common advice also includes: learn one framework but understand what it does underneath; focus on retrieval quality in RAG; read official documentation over outdated tutorials; contribute to open source; and expect the role to require backend and deployment skills. Discussions also warn against hype: many AI engineering jobs are software engineering jobs with AI components, so treat the fundamentals as non-negotiable.

What is the LLM engineer roadmap?

An LLM engineer specialises in building and optimising systems around large language models. Beyond the AI engineer basics, go deeper into tokenisation, context management, advanced retrieval, fine-tuning, evaluation, inference optimisation and serving.

Track:

  • Transformer basics: attention, context windows, sampling.
  • Advanced RAG: hybrid search, reranking, query rewriting, graph approaches.
  • Fine-tuning: SFT with LoRA and QLoRA, preference tuning basics, dataset building.
  • Evaluation: task metrics, faithfulness, regression tests, human review.
  • Serving: quantisation, batching, vLLM or llama.cpp, latency and cost.
  • Safety: prompt injection defences and output validation.

Prove skills with a RAG system and a fine-tuned model, each with evaluation results.

What is the roadmap for an agentic AI engineer?

An agentic AI engineer builds systems where models plan and take actions through tools. Start with solid LLM and RAG skills, then learn tool calling, workflow orchestration, memory, the Model Context Protocol, evaluation of multi-step tasks and safety controls.

Track:

  • Tool calling with structured schemas and error handling.
  • Workflow patterns: chaining, routing, parallelisation, orchestrator and workers.
  • Frameworks such as LangGraph or provider agent SDKs.
  • MCP servers to connect tools and data.
  • Guardrails: permissions, budgets, approvals, logging.
  • Evaluating agents on task success, cost and safety.

Build an agent that completes a real multi-step task, such as triaging support tickets, with logs and limits.

What is the roadmap for an AI automation engineer?

An AI automation engineer automates business processes with workflow tools and AI steps. Learn a platform such as n8n, Make, Zapier or Power Automate, APIs and webhooks, data handling, LLM prompting for classification and extraction, and reliable error handling.

Track:

  • APIs, JSON, webhooks and authentication basics.
  • One workflow platform deeply, plus scripting in Python or JavaScript.
  • AI steps: classification, extraction, summarisation with validation.
  • Document processing with OCR and structured output.
  • Reliability: retries, logging, alerts, human approval steps.
  • Measuring impact: time saved and error rates.

A portfolio of three automations with measured results is the strongest proof for this role.

What is the AI security engineer roadmap?

AI security engineers protect AI systems and use AI for security. Build general security foundations, then learn AI-specific risks: prompt injection, data leakage, insecure tool use, model and data supply chains, and abuse of AI features, using frameworks like the OWASP Top 10 for LLM applications.

Track:

  • Security basics: web vulnerabilities, authentication, cloud security, threat modelling.
  • LLM risks: direct and indirect prompt injection, jailbreaks, sensitive data exposure.
  • Agent risks: excessive permissions, unsafe tool calls, insecure output handling.
  • Red-teaming and evaluation of AI systems.
  • Privacy and compliance for AI data, including biometric data.

Practise by red-teaming your own RAG or agent projects and documenting fixes.

What is a roadmap for BCA students on roadmap.sh?

roadmap.sh does not have a degree-specific BCA roadmap, but BCA students can follow its role roadmaps, such as Backend, Full Stack or AI Engineer, alongside coursework. BCA covers programming, databases and web development, a good base for AI engineering.

A three-year plan I share with students:

  • Year 1: programming fundamentals, Git, web basics, a first deployed project.
  • Year 2: backend with FastAPI or Node, SQL, an LLM-powered app, hackathons.
  • Year 3: RAG and agents, internships, a capstone with real users, interview preparation.

Employers hiring AI engineers care about what you can build; deployed projects and hackathon results let BCA students compete with any degree.
