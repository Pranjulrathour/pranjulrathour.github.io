How to get a hackathon certificate, add it to LinkedIn, and make a winning hackathon PPT

Key takeaways

  • Certificates come from completing the event's requirements, usually a valid project submission, and arrive through the platform or email.
  • Add certificates to LinkedIn under Licenses and certifications or Honors and awards, with the project link.
  • A winning hackathon PPT is short: problem, user, solution, demo, tech, impact, team.
  • For Smart India Hackathon, use the official template exactly and focus on feasibility and impact.
  • Use AI tools as assistants within the event's rules; judges reward understanding, not generated code.

To get a hackathon certificate, register on the official platform, complete the event's requirements, usually submitting a valid project by the deadline, and download the certificate from the platform or your email after results. Winners get merit certificates; many events also give participation certificates. I have won first prizes at Changethon 2025, Product Genesis at Vividhotsava 2025 and BYTEBATTLE by MeetKats, so this page covers certificates, LinkedIn, free online events, templates, winning PPTs, Smart India Hackathon slides, getting in, and using AI.

How do I get a hackathon certificate?

Register with accurate details, form or join a team, submit a complete project before the deadline, and follow any final steps such as feedback forms. Certificates are then issued through the hosting platform, such as Devfolio or Unstop, or emailed by organisers.

Also asked as: how to get hackathon certificate · does hackathon provide participation certificate · how to get in hackathon

Check your name spelling at registration, since certificates use it. Some events issue certificates only to teams that submit working projects or reach certain rounds. If yours does not arrive within a few weeks, contact organisers through official channels with your team name and registration ID. Keep certificates with your project links so they support your resume.

Do hackathons provide participation certificates?

Many do. Most online hackathons on platforms like Devfolio, Unstop and Major League Hacking, and many college events, give participation certificates to teams that submit valid projects. Some events give them only to finalists or winners.

Check the event page or FAQ before registering. Participation certificates show involvement, but recruiters value the project more: a working demo, code repository and short write-up of what you built and learned. Winning or finalist certificates carry more weight. Collect certificates, but focus your energy on building something worth showing.

How do I add a hackathon certificate to LinkedIn?

Go to your LinkedIn profile, choose "Add profile section", and add it under "Licenses and certifications" for participation or completion, or under "Honors and awards" for wins. Include the organiser, date, credential URL if provided, and a project link.

Also asked as: how to add hackathon certificate in linkedin

Better still, write a post about the project: the problem, what you built, a demo video and what you learned, tagging teammates and organisers. Posts reach more people than profile entries and show communication skills. Add the project in the "Projects" section too, linking to code and demo. Keep entries accurate; do not list a win as first place if you were a finalist.

What are the best free online hackathons with certificates?

Free online hackathons with certificates are regularly listed on Devfolio, Unstop, Devpost and Major League Hacking, including global student events and company-sponsored challenges. Many university fests also run free online rounds.

Also asked as: best free online hackathon with certificate

Choose events that offer mentors, clear judging criteria and a theme you care about. Major League Hacking's member events and Devpost's open hackathons often give participation recognition for valid submissions. Read the rules: some certificates need a minimum submission quality or a demo video. Mixing a few online events with offline ones gives both portfolio projects and networking.

Is there a hackathon certificate template?

Organisers can design certificates with tools such as Canva, Google Slides or Figma, many of which offer free certificate templates. Include the event name, participant name, achievement, date, organiser logos and signatures, and a verification ID or URL.

For participants, never create your own certificate for an event you did not complete; fake certificates are easy to detect and can end opportunities. For organisers, generate certificates automatically from your registration sheet using mail-merge tools or scripts, and host verification links so recruiters can confirm authenticity. Platforms like Devfolio and Unstop can issue them directly.

How do I make a hackathon PPT?

Keep it short and visual: one slide each for the problem, the user, your solution, a demo screenshot or video, how it works technically, impact and next steps, and the team. Aim for six to ten slides you can present in three to five minutes.

Also asked as: how to make hackathon ppt

Tips:

  • Start with a real person's problem, not your tech stack.
  • Use screenshots of your working product rather than mock-ups.
  • Show architecture in one simple diagram.
  • Quantify impact where possible.
  • Keep text minimal; you are the narrator.
  • Rehearse with a timer, and prepare a backup demo video.

The deck supports the demo; the demo wins.

What makes the best hackathon PPT?

The best hackathon presentations tell a clear story: a specific problem, a working solution shown live, evidence it works, and why it matters. Judges remember a demo that clearly solves a real problem, not slide animations.

Strong decks share traits: a one-line pitch on the title slide, a relatable user story, a live or recorded demo early, simple architecture, honest limitations and a credible next step. KrishGyan's winning pitch at Changethon 2025 centred on one farmer asking for advice in their own voice and language over WhatsApp, and the demo showed exactly that flow working.

How do I make a Smart India Hackathon PPT?

Download the official idea presentation template from the Smart India Hackathon site and follow it exactly, keeping slide order and limits. Typically it covers the problem statement ID and title, your proposed solution, technical approach, feasibility and viability, impact and benefits, and references.

Also asked as: how to make smart india hackathon ppt

Tips for SIH:

  • Address the exact problem statement given by the ministry or organisation.
  • Explain how your solution works with a clear diagram.
  • Show feasibility: data sources, tech stack, risks and how you will handle them.
  • Describe measurable impact on the intended users.
  • Cite research and existing solutions, explaining your improvement.

Internal college rounds often select teams based on this PPT, so clarity matters.

How do I get into a hackathon?

Find events on Devfolio, Unstop, Major League Hacking, Devpost or college fest pages, register before the deadline, and form a team with complementary skills, such as front end, back end, design and domain knowledge. Selective events may ask for an application or idea submission.

To improve selection chances, show past projects or GitHub activity, write a thoughtful application explaining what you want to build and learn, and apply to beginner-friendly events first. If you have no team, use the event's team-matching channels or join solo where allowed. After your first event, it gets much easier to find teammates and opportunities.

Can I get AI to do my hackathon project?

You can use AI tools as assistants if the rules allow, for coding help, debugging, design ideas and drafting slides, but having AI build the whole project usually backfires. Judges ask detailed questions, and you need to understand and explain your work.

Also asked as: how to get ai to do my hackathon project

Read each event's rules on AI use and starter code; some require disclosure. Use AI to move faster on boilerplate, then spend your time on the problem, user experience and the core logic you can defend. If your project uses AI as a feature, explain your model choice, prompts, evaluation and limitations. That understanding is what impresses judges.
