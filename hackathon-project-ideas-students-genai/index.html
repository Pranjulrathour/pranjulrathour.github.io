The best hackathon ideas start from a real problem you understand, scoped small enough to demo in a day or two. Winning projects often combine a clear user need with a working prototype. I won first prizes at Changethon 2025 with KrishGyan, which gives farming advice in a farmer's voice and language over WhatsApp, and at BYTEBATTLE with Annapurna, a surplus-food coordination app, so this page shares ideas for beginners, students, healthcare, testing, companies and GenAI, plus where to find more.

What are good hackathon project ideas for beginners?

Pick one simple, useful feature you can finish: a study planner that suggests schedules, a campus lost-and-found board, a bill splitter for hostel groups, a habit tracker with reminders, or a chatbot that answers questions from your college handbook.

Beginners succeed by scoping tightly and using templates, starter kits and familiar tools. Aim for a working demo with one smooth flow rather than many half-built features. Add a small AI touch, such as summarising notes or classifying requests, using an API with clear prompts. Deploy early so the demo works on any laptop. Practise the demo several times before judging. The goal of a first hackathon is to finish, learn and present confidently, not to build the most complex system.

What are the best hackathon ideas for students?

Strong student ideas solve campus problems: canteen queue prediction, notes sharing with search, QR-based attendance, accessible campus maps, alumni mentor matching, mental health check-ins with resources, and club event management with reminders.

Because you live these problems, you can validate them quickly with classmates and demonstrate real user feedback, which judges value. Collect a few quotes or survey results before the event if allowed. Keep privacy in mind, especially for health or attendance data. Projects that continue after the hackathon, gaining real users, become strong portfolio pieces and sometimes startups. Several of my products began as student hackathon builds, which shows how far a focused idea can go.

What are some unique hackathon project ideas?

Unique ideas often combine a local problem with an unexpected channel: voice-first assistants in regional languages over WhatsApp, surplus food coordination between restaurants and shelters, tools for local artisans to sell online, or offline-first apps for low-connectivity areas.

Also asked as: new to hackathon want some ideas

KrishGyan and Annapurna came from this approach: farmers prefer voice in their own language on familiar apps, and food waste and hunger coexist in the same neighbourhoods. Look for gaps where existing apps assume English, fast internet or smartphones. Talk to people affected before building. Uniqueness alone does not win; judges reward ideas that are original, clearly needed and demonstrably working, with a believable path to real use.

What are good non-technical hackathon ideas?

Non-technical hackathons and tracks welcome ideas such as policy proposals, awareness campaigns, service design for public offices, business models for social enterprises, educational content, and prototypes built with no-code tools such as Glide or Bubble.

Also asked as: non technical hackathon ideas

Strong submissions show research, user journeys, clear problem statements and measurable impact, presented through mockups, storyboards or simple clickable prototypes. Ideathons often run this way, judging thinking rather than code, and clear storytelling often decides the winner. Non-technical participants also strengthen technical teams by handling research, design, pitching and user testing. If you want to build without coding, no-code tools and spreadsheet-based apps let you demonstrate a working flow, which makes ideas more convincing.

What are the best hackathon ideas in healthcare?

Promising healthcare ideas include medicine reminders in local languages, blood donor matching, hospital bed and appointment availability dashboards, tools supporting community health workers in data collection, mental health journaling with resources, and accessibility aids for patients.

Healthcare projects must respect privacy and safety: avoid giving diagnoses, include clear disclaimers, store minimal data and follow consent practices. Symptom checkers should direct users to professionals rather than replace them. Talk to doctors, nurses or health workers to understand real workflows; judges value domain validation, so partner with a medical student or professional if possible. Simple solutions that fit existing processes, such as reminders via SMS or WhatsApp, often have more impact than complex AI that clinicians would not adopt.

What are good software testing hackathon ideas?

Testing hackathon ideas include automating tests for an open-source project, detecting flaky tests from CI history, generating test cases with AI and validating them, accessibility testing tools, load testing dashboards and bug bashes on real applications.

Other ideas: mutation testing to measure test quality, visual regression testing for websites, contract testing between microservices, and tools that turn bug reports into reproducible test cases. Testing hackathons reward measurable results, such as bugs found, coverage improved or test time reduced, so track metrics from the start. Pair developers with testers to share perspectives. Contributing tests to open-source projects also leaves lasting value and builds your GitHub profile with merged pull requests.

What are good hackathon ideas for a company?

Company hackathons work best around real business problems: internal tools that save time, automation of repetitive processes, AI assistants over internal documents, customer pain points from support data, developer experience improvements and accessibility or sustainability projects.

Also asked as: hackathon ideas for company

Set themes linked to company goals, form cross-functional teams mixing engineering, design, operations and sales, and judge on business impact and feasibility, not just technical flair. Provide sandbox access to APIs and data with proper security. A RAG assistant over internal policies, similar in approach to RAG.NextUpgrad, is a popular and practical theme. Plan how winning projects will continue afterwards, since abandoned prototypes discourage future participation.

Where can you find hackathon project ideas on GitHub?

Search GitHub topics such as hackathon and hackathon-ideas, browse curated awesome lists, and study winning projects linked from Devpost's project gallery and MLH events, many of which publish their code on GitHub.

Also asked as: hackathon project ideas github

Studying past winners shows what judges value: clear problem statements, working demos, good READMEs and polished presentations. Use them for inspiration and learning, not copying; most hackathons require original work during the event, and judges notice reused projects. Note which APIs and frameworks teams used, and how they scoped features to fit the time limit. Star useful repositories to revisit later, and keep a personal list of ideas and problems you notice, ready for the next event.

New to hackathons: how do you come up with ideas?

Start from problems you or people around you face, then check the event's themes, sponsor tools and judging criteria. Pick an idea you can explain in one sentence and demo in two minutes, with one core feature.

Brainstorm with teammates, list ten problems, then score each on impact, feasibility and fit with the theme. Talk to a mentor early to sanity-check scope. Avoid ideas that need data you cannot access or hardware you do not have. Prepare reusable pieces beforehand where rules allow, such as a starter template and deployment setup. Most importantly, choose something you care about, since motivation carries you through the late hours.

Where can you find lots of GenAI hackathon ideas?

Large lists of GenAI hackathon ideas circulate online, but the best ideas come from real problems. Strong GenAI themes include RAG assistants over documents, tutors in local languages, voice agents, document extraction, meeting summarisers, accessibility tools and agents that fill forms with human approval.

Also asked as: over 800 genai hackathon ideas

Ground GenAI projects in evidence and safety: cite sources, handle uncertainty and avoid hallucinated answers in sensitive domains. RAG.NextUpgrad's pattern of hybrid retrieval, citations and a confidence gate translates well to hackathon builds. Use free tiers and local models to control costs. Judges increasingly see generic chatbots, so differentiate with a specific user, domain data and measurable improvement, such as time saved compared with the current process.
