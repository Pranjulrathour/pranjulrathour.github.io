Become an AI engineer: maths, data science, BSc, after 12th, timelines and 2026 paths

Key takeaways

  • Maths, data science and B.Sc graduates can become AI engineers by adding software skills and projects.
  • After 12th commerce, choose BCA or open-stream programmes and learn Python and statistics early.
  • Expect three to four years via a degree, or twelve to eighteen months of focused self-study.
  • Without a CS degree, shipped projects, open source and internships replace credentials.
  • Andrew Ng's and Anthropic's free resources teach fundamentals and practical building.

You can become an AI engineer from many starting points: a maths, data science or computer science degree, commerce or science after 12th, or self-study without a CS degree. What matters is fundamentals plus shipped projects. My own path runs through a BCA, leading engineering at SCULT INDIA since 2024 and shipping five NextUpgrad AI apps in 2026, so this page covers degree routes, after-12th options, timelines and 2026 paths, including Andrew Ng's and Anthropic's learning resources.

Can you become an engineer with a maths degree?

Yes, as a software or AI engineer. Mathematics graduates bring strong foundations in linear algebra, probability and optimisation, which machine learning relies on. Licensed engineering roles, such as civil or electrical, usually require an accredited engineering degree instead.

Also asked as: can you become an engineer with a math degree

To move into AI engineering, add programming in Python, data structures, databases, Git and software practices, then build machine learning and LLM projects. In India, maths postgraduates can pursue an MCA, or an M.Tech through GATE where institutes accept them, to formalise a computing qualification. Many strong ML researchers and engineers studied mathematics first. Show employers your ability through deployed projects and clear write-ups that explain the maths behind your models.

Can you become an AI engineer with a data science degree?

Yes. A data science degree covers statistics, machine learning and data handling, which form much of AI engineering. The main gap is usually software engineering: building APIs, testing, deployment and working with production systems.

Close that gap by building end-to-end applications: a FastAPI service around a model, a RAG system with evaluation, or a fine-tuned model deployed with Docker. Learn Git workflows, cloud basics and monitoring. Data science graduates often move into AI engineering through ML engineer or applied scientist roles. Your statistical training becomes an advantage in evaluating models properly, a skill many purely software-trained engineers lack, and it helps you spot misleading metrics.

Can you become an AI engineer after a B.Sc in computer science?

Yes. A B.Sc in computer science provides programming, algorithms, databases and systems fundamentals. Add machine learning, deep learning, LLM application skills and real projects, and you can apply for AI engineering roles directly or after a master's.

Also asked as: can i become ai engineer after bsc computer science

Some employers prefer B.Tech graduates for campus hiring, but skills and projects increasingly matter more, especially at startups. Strengthen your profile with internships, hackathons and open-source contributions. An M.Sc, MCA or M.Tech can help for research-oriented roles or companies with strict degree filters. My own path through a BCA, a similar three-year computing degree, shows that building and shipping real AI products can matter more than the degree title.

How do you become an AI engineer after 12th commerce?

Choose a computing degree that accepts commerce students, such as BCA, which many colleges offer with or without class 12 maths, or online programmes such as IIT Madras's BS in Data Science and Applications, which admits students from any stream.

Also asked as: how to become ai engineer after 12th commerce

Check each programme's eligibility carefully, since some require maths in class 12. Meanwhile, start learning Python, statistics and basic mathematics for machine learning, filling gaps left by commerce subjects. Build small data projects using business data you understand, such as sales analysis or demand prediction, which suit your background. Over the degree, progress to machine learning, LLM applications and internships. Commerce students bring business understanding that helps when building AI for real companies.

How do you become an AI engineer in India after 12th science?

With physics, chemistry and maths, take JEE Main or state exams for B.Tech in computer science, AI or data science, or choose BCA or B.Sc computer science. Start Python and projects alongside your degree, not after it.

Also asked as: how to become ai engineer in india after 12th science

Follow a yearly plan: first year, programming, Git and maths; second year, data structures, machine learning and a first internship; third year, deep learning, LLM applications and a flagship project; final year, specialisation and placement preparation. Join hackathons and tech clubs early. Students who studied biology without maths should check programme eligibility and may need bridge courses in mathematics. At the VSICS induction, I shared this kind of three-year roadmap with first-years.

How many years does it take to become an AI engineer after 12th?

Typically three to four years: a three-year BCA or B.Sc, or a four-year B.Tech, during which you build skills and projects. Some students reach junior AI roles before graduating through internships and freelance work.

Also asked as: how many years to become ai engineer after 12th

The degree sets the timeline for formal qualification, but readiness depends on what you build. Students who start projects in their first year can be internship-ready by the second, while those relying only on coursework may need extra months after graduating. A master's adds one to two years for research or specialised roles. Treat the time as a structured runway: steady learning, two or three serious projects and real-world experience before final year.

How long does it take to become an AI engineer from scratch?

With no programming background, expect about twelve to eighteen months of consistent study and building to reach junior AI engineering level: three months of Python and fundamentals, six months of machine learning and LLM applications, then projects and job preparation.

Also asked as: how long to become an ai engineer from scratch

The pace depends on hours per week, prior maths knowledge and the quality of practice. Building real projects shortens the path more than watching courses. Milestones help: a deployed web API, a RAG application with evaluation, a fine-tuned small model and an agent with tools. Join communities for feedback and accountability. Career switchers can often move faster by combining AI skills with domain knowledge from previous work, such as finance or healthcare.

How do you become an AI engineer in 2026 without a CS degree?

Build proof instead of credentials: master Python, fundamentals and LLM application skills, then ship three to five public projects with evaluation, contribute to open source, and pursue internships, freelance work or startup roles that value demonstrated ability.

Also asked as: how to become an ai engineer in 2026 (without a cs degree

In 2026, AI engineering focuses heavily on building with models: RAG, agents, evaluation, fine-tuning and deployment, which are learnable through free resources and practice. Write about what you build, since clear explanations signal understanding. Network through hackathons, meetups and online communities. Some large employers still filter by degree, so target startups and teams that hire on portfolios first. Certifications help a little, but working software and measured results help much more.

How does Andrew Ng suggest becoming an AI engineer?

Andrew Ng's advice, shared through DeepLearning.AI and his free ebook How to Build Your Career in AI, centres on three steps: learn foundational skills, work on projects of increasing scope, and find a job or create opportunities.

Also asked as: how to become an ai engineer in 2026 andrew ng

He recommends learning machine learning and deep learning fundamentals, software development, and mathematics relevant to AI, then building projects that solve real problems, starting small and growing in ambition. His courses, from the Machine Learning Specialization to short courses on LLMs and agents, provide structure. He also emphasises community, mentorship and continuous learning, since the field changes quickly. Combine his courses with independent projects so learning turns into a portfolio.

How do Anthropic's resources help you become an AI engineer?

Anthropic publishes free learning material for building with Claude: courses on the API, prompt engineering and tool use on GitHub, an interactive prompt engineering tutorial, documentation, cookbooks and guides such as Building effective agents.

Also asked as: how to become an ai engineer in 2026 anthropic

These resources teach practical AI engineering skills: calling models through APIs, designing prompts, using tools, retrieval, evaluation and agent patterns, plus the Model Context Protocol for connecting tools. Work through them by building projects, such as a document assistant or a coding helper, rather than only reading. The same principles apply across providers, so pair Anthropic's material with other ecosystems, including Hugging Face for open models, to stay provider-agnostic and employable.
